## Context

Remembering choices can improve continuity but can also manufacture success from selection frequency or completed actions.

## Knowledge at the time

History records recursive-learning infrastructure, outcome-aware navigation, staged review, action grammar, closure repairs, and SQLite hardening.

## Decision

Maintain explicit selection and outcome boundaries, with action authority attached to the visible bounded choice rather than to retention.

## Action and result

Controls and storage changes were committed. Their existence does not establish lower cognitive load, better judgment, or a completed natural-use cohort.

## Interpretation

Learning infrastructure needs evidence of effects, not just records of what the operator selected.

## Remaining obligation

The choice workflow owns later outcome review. A future reviewer must use bounded post-action observations before treating a selection or closure as successful learning.
