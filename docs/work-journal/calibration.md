## Review method

Check each new entry for six recoverable answers: decision, rationale, evidence
limit, result, unresolved obligation, and authority boundary. Reject an entry
that replaces an unknown outcome with success, treats current wording as past
knowledge, invents a decision owner, or turns a comparison into a proven lesson.

The Letters Orientation example is preserved byte-for-byte, rather than
rewritten to fit the new headings. Its existing sections provide the answers.
New entries use the standard seven-section form. Structural checks establish
presence and links; the author's source comparison establishes the bounded
editorial review and is not an independent evaluation of usefulness.

## Privacy and attribution review

- Grace Gems entries contain stewardship/control distinctions, not private
  commercial evidence or a claim that a pilot was run.
- Mentorship content is limited to timing and coordination; private responses,
  named assessments, and personal or financial details are not reproduced.
- Mira Seed is described from local parent-side records only; specifications
  and recorded baseline findings are attributed, not certified afresh.
- Historical obligations are dated accounts and links to their owners, not a
  second task queue or a current authority surface.
