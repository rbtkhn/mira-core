## Context

## Knowledge at the time

History records the era scaffold, private text store, text validation, era architecture requirements, and v1.0 seal.

## Decision

## Action and result

The release seal covers Ancient, Medieval, Colonial, and Industrial lineage. The README explicitly excludes a fresh replay of every private payload and excludes Digital from v1.0 scope.

## Interpretation

A seal is meaningful only when its coverage and limits travel with it.
