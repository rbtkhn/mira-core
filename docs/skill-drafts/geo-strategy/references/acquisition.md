# Geopolitical acquisition and pending work

This is the former Tower acquisition contract, now owned by Geo-Strategy.
Mind availability and ordinary strategic conversation never activate retrieval.
Explicit current geopolitical work keeps its existing bounded acquisition authority.
Recover the named inquiry through Mind-owned Strategy Notebook first.

## Process

### Newsletter continuity on explicit current geopolitical entry

For explicit current geopolitical entry, first consume the recovered inquiry and its complete
relevant contributions, corrections, and qualifications. Current operator focus
controls; the latest recorded inquiry is not proof of current activity. Mind
governs attention and judgment throughout. Known owners retrieve directly; use
Mira Memory only for missing ownership or cross-carrier reconciliation, without
a mandatory status call or unified context pack.

Then follow [Newsletter Capture](../newsletter-capture/SKILL.md). After its
supervised pilot passes, current entry authorizes retrieval of approved
publications and sequential admission of complete geopolitical articles using
`newsletter-capture tower-refresh`. Before pilot acceptance, report readiness
and continue supported strategic work. The operator may explicitly skip refresh.
Historical inquiries, architectural discussions and quoted room names do not
activate mailbox access. This does not expand YouTube discovery authority.

Read `geo-strategy-acquisition pending` after refresh. Its separate `newsletters` projection reports
capture coverage; existing source-version accounting governs landed analysis.
Reconcile older pending work and explicit deferrals. Select by consequence and
inquiry relevance, not receipt order, subscription, or publication volume. Do not
turn a capture receipt into a considered source or independent corroboration.
Mailbox failure is a coverage gap, not a clean survey, and does not block analysis
of available sources. Retain Geo-Strategy and Library composition requirements.
No automatic Journal, note, identity, or recursive-learning retention follows.

### Current geopolitics: survey the window

An explicit request to complete current or pending geopolitics work authorizes
one bounded sequence: recover the inquiry, survey today's curated daily channels,
capture eligible transcripts with `mira-youtube`, land them through Archive Intake,
read and analyze through Geo-Strategy, and append a Strategy Notebook contribution.
Continue eligible rows without repeated approval; preserve each owner's safeguards.
This does not apply to generic invitations, room mentions, historical inquiries,
or source-bound readings. Those retain their stated scope.

Run `tools/run.ps1 geo-strategy-acquisition survey-plan --date DATE --json` first. It reads the
channel index and existing capture evidence; it performs no browser actions.
Use today's dated-calendar date plus recorded pending work. Do not discover
older dates without explicit catch-up scope. Empty backlog is not checked channels.
Follow its `mira-youtube daily-check` and `browser-coverage` commands. Inspect
Subscriptions first when authenticated, verify candidate watch pages, and record
channel receipts with fresh access context. Missing access is a gap, not an empty
survey. `mira-youtube discover` reports supplied evidence; it does not browse or
extract transcripts. YouTube supplies attributed accounts, not verified world facts.

Attach transcripts to routed capture records before intake. Already-landed material
bypasses capture and admission; changed versions retain existing source-version accounting.
Unavailable transcripts or intake problems do not block unrelated eligible sources.
Report survey coverage, captured, landed, analyzed, excluded, and unresolved work
separately. No factual adjudication, forecast resolution, channel promotion, account
change, or publication is implied.

For this route set `entry_mode: current-geopolitics` and include `survey` in the
close input using the returned `survey_binding`: `date`, ordered `channel_slugs`,
`receipts`, `status`, and `gaps`. Close validates current scope
and receipt hashes. An incomplete survey permits a qualified contribution with
the exact gaps; it does not permit a completion claim. Legacy contributions need
no migration. A saved contribution retry remains immutable and reusable.

`geo-strategy-acquisition pending --date DATE --json` is read-only and reports acquisition, intake,
analysis, and coverage gaps. `--catch-up` includes older archived sources.
An absent activation inventory is a gap, never a clean queue. One authorized
installation runs `geo-strategy-acquisition activate --json` to preserve the private baseline:
existing unresolved capture targets plus subsequently new or changed sources.
It does not mark historical sources as analyzed. Activation refuses incomplete
readers. No automatic capture, backfill, or monitor is created.

Route transcript acquisition through YouTube Capture and admission through
Archive Intake. Landed geopolitical analysis uses Geo-Strategy. Singularity
sources retain their own provenance and cannot substitute for Geo source IDs.
A generic strategic inquiry does not itself admit bodies or authorize external actions.
The explicit current-work request above supplies bounded intake authority.
For an explicitly selected Dream batch, continue authorized reversible processing
through these owners; report unavailable material without inventing completion.


Close substantive named Notebook work through Mira Mind. Source disposition and survey checks remain mandatory for their existing modes. Dream consumes completed results; it never composes an assessment.
