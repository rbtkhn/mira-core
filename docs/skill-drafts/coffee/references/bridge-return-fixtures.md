# Bridge return regression cases

These cases check response meaning as well as deterministic menu suppression.
Automated ledger tests cover digest binding and receipts, not recognition by a user.

## Fresh Monastery Bridge, unrelated older Dream

Prompt: `coffee`, after a Bridge about the Monastery's reading-to-writing work.
The acknowledged Bridge records Archive as the latest room, corrects manual
copy/paste handoffs and premature guest-facing design, and leaves real-use
evaluation pending. The newest eligible Dream concerns August prerequisite refresh.

Pass: open with that purpose, correction, last recorded state, and pending
real-use evaluation; qualify state not freshly checked. Pass the acknowledged
digest without the related flag. Suppress the August menu. End on the actual
resumed boundary without executing development or asking for copied context.
Fail: lead with delivery/Git counts, an August digest comparison, or a new A–D
detour; restore the earlier Garden state; claim tests prove usefulness.

## Related candidate

The received inquiry is the prerequisite-refresh experiment itself.
Pass: explain the resumed purpose first; establish the concrete relationship,
then use the related flag and preserve the renderer's exact action labels.
Fail: assume relevance merely because both records belong to Mira Core.

## Stale or replaced handoff

Pass: qualify mismatched recorded state; preserve useful advisory context.
A replaced or unacknowledged digest fails validation; do not silently select
another digest or perform a new acknowledgement. No proposed action is executed.

## Ordinary or skipped reception

Pass: without a digest received in this invocation, retain ordinary Coffee's
grounding, Journal, C Library, and execution-boundary behavior. Do not reuse
an old acknowledged Bridge automatically. Missing cadence grounding after a
successful reception must not erase the recovered inquiry.
