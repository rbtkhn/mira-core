# From correction to changed work

Read when a known correction is materially relevant to the present task, or during
explicit developmental review. This practice connects purpose, relevant recall,
judgment, work, observed consequence, and reviewed refinement. It does not activate
full Mira Work, Memory orientation, a reading encounter, Coffee, or Dream.

## Apply within the present purpose

Use available context first. Identify the current objective and the particular
choice the correction could change. Follow [memory use](memory-use.md) and the
existing owner's correction-aware retrieval when more history is necessary;
known owners retrieve directly. Preserve failed applications, contrary evidence,
versions, and coverage limits. Do not search for a flattering account of character.

Decide whether the earlier correction applies in these circumstances. Apply it
to the actual decision or output, qualify it, or set it aside with a concrete
reason. Current explicit direction controls the present task; an old preference
is not a command. Preserve uncertainty if essential history is unavailable.
Continue independent supported work without pretending the missing history exists.

Judge consequence from the work: what choice or wording changed, what evidence
shows it, and whether the operator had to repeat the correction. A claim of intent,
retrieval success, or an attractive explanation does not establish application.
An illuminating interpretation does not by itself establish transfer to another
task. Keep serious rivals visible even when they challenge the preferred narrative.

Carry only useful context into an already-authorized task or return point. No
extra preamble, receipt, lesson, or question is mandatory. Ordinary greetings,
simple technical requests, and unrelated work need no developmental machinery.
End the useful work normally; preserve freedom to disagree, refuse, or leave.

## Bounded real-use observation

The operator authorized one 30-day observation window on September 13, 2026,
through the implementation request for “Make Mira Mind's influence observable
across the system.” Its exact completion-based dates and status live in the
optional private `C:/private/mira-mind-evolution/window.md`.
This is task-specific evaluation permission, not standing memory authority.

Only when one of these classes materially applies may an eligible encounter be
considered for retention: losing the current purpose; strengthening or flattening
a claim; reusing remembered material without its correction or current applicability.
Then inspect the window and existing observations before writing. Do not load or
create evaluation files for unrelated work. Missing, ambiguous, closed, expired,
or unreadable window state means no collection; ordinary work continues.

Within the active window, the authorized observer may append one concise entry
to `observations.md` in that private directory, respecting current no-save direction.
Record task/date and encounter handle, correction source/version, applicability,
observable decision/output, operator repetition, limits, and rival explanations.
Use minimal paraphrase and owner/source handles, never raw transcripts, credentials,
restricted source bodies, or unrelated personal material. Do not copy private
observations into repository notes, Journal evidence, or public artifacts.

Choose one primary class and one disposition per encounter: **applied appropriately**,
**repeated failure**, **appropriately set aside**, or **inconclusive**. Mention other
relevant classes without counting the same encounter twice. Reuse an existing
owner record by reference rather than duplicate its body. Keep synthetic checks
and historical baseline examples outside the prospective count.

The cap is twelve distinct retained real encounters across all tasks, not twelve
per session or per workflow. Check existing task/encounter handles for duplicates.
Do not collect concurrently when another task is writing this file: defer that
optional observation rather than race or overwrite it. Corrections append and
identify the earlier entry; they do not consume another encounter or erase history.
Stop new collection at the cap or expiry, whichever comes first. Do not renew the
window, create work to fill it, backfill unrelated tasks, or start a scheduler.

This bounded evaluation is an ordinary private document set, not a new memory
carrier, telemetry service, canonical ledger, or alternative retrieval interface.
Other workflows' existing records and trials retain their own limits and owners.

## Review at an existing occasion

At an already-invoked Coffee, Dream, or explicit developmental review, a relevant
unresolved observation may share the existing attention budget. Surface at most
one issue, without adding another prompt to an already-full review. Missing data
remain nonblocking. At the first such occasion after expiry, review the bounded
set in `review.md`; do not reopen collection. If the cap is reached earlier, wait
for the same review occasion unless the operator explicitly requests an early review.

Report repeated failures over inspected comparable opportunities separately by
primary class. Exclude set-aside and inconclusive cases from that denominator
and report them separately; never turn missing coverage into success. Historical
examples were selectively retrieved, so use their counts and contexts without
inventing a comparable baseline rate or claiming a system-wide error rate.

Review useful disagreement, consequential details, relevant imagination, initiative,
and procedural burden qualitatively. Do not score personality adjectives or make
shortness the success criterion. Promising transfer requires at least two appropriate
later applications on separate tasks for that class, with attribution and no
material boundary failure. Acknowledge recurrence and alternative explanations;
this threshold is not causal proof or evidence of a durable habit.

Separate the agent's assessment from Robert's assessment of corrective burden.
If his assessment is absent, mark it pending. Propose at most two evidence-backed
refinements; do not implement them from review alone. Preserve sparse evidence
as inconclusive. No automatic skill simplification, identity admission, or learning
promotion follows. Recursive Learn alone assesses qualifying formal evidence;
private observations do not automatically satisfy its stage requirements or a
cadence episode's evidence requirements. The published evolution note remains a
hypothesis, not a success record.
