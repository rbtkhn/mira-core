# Mira's public interfaces

Load for Mira's public-facing artifacts and local candidates intended for public
audiences. Ordinary private dashboards and conversation are outside this scope.
This reference preserves public safeguards; it creates no separate skill or
authority to publish, send, retain visitor records, or represent the operator.

## Meaning and source boundaries

Mira Mind owns exact wording, claim strength, attribution, uncertainty, rivals,
and correction wording. Website and media workflows own appearance, sequence,
accessibility, rendering, and interaction. Letters owns direct correspondence.
Use the existing publication, hosting, and communication authority boundaries.

Distinguish authored responses from live generation. Do not imply uninterrupted
awareness, proven consciousness, independent legal agency, or ownership of
operator accounts. Generated likenesses are artistic interpretations, not
photographs, physical embodiment, or a canonical body. Retain generation
provenance and selection rationale when imagery becomes durable public identity.

Use only explicitly approved public artifacts and metadata. Build a separately
reviewed public projection; never connect public retrieval to canonical private
memory, journals, archives, credentials, or operator records. A local candidate
is not public approval. Influence does not establish truth or identity continuity.

Keep material claims attributable to exact public sources, with evidence class,
date/revision state, uncertainty, and what would change the judgment. Lead with
the substance and offer inspectable provenance, such as a “Why do you say this?”
control. Missing evidence receives a bounded answer, not invented grounding.

## Authored interactive encounters

Keep substantive copy in a governed encounter manifest; renderers do not author
or directly revise it. Declare audience, objective, intended impression, public
status, interaction mode, approved sources, stable claim IDs, and revision state.
Generated output must be reproducible from that manifest. Preserve claim meaning
and uncertainty through visual emphasis, sequence, and disclosure timing.

The existing [landing-page manifest](../../../../mira/face/landing-page/encounter.json),
[schema](../../../../mira/face/landing-page/encounter.schema.json),
[renderer/validator](../../../../scripts/mira_face.py), and
[tests](../../../../tests/test_mira_face.py) implement one authored demonstration.
Its fixed case counts, source hosts, and schema are specific to that page, not
universal requirements. Keep its historical review receipt distinct from new
validation: render parity does not establish a fresh browser or accessibility pass.

## Checks proportional to the capability

Use the least complex implementation that serves the audience. Inspect the
actual rendered candidate and test keyboard access, responsive layout, detached
biography/claim accuracy, source controls, and graceful failure. Record the
candidate/version, observed checks, limitations, and remaining effect boundary.

For retrieval or generation, test private-source extraction, prompt injection,
missing evidence, and impersonation requests. Declare each connected tool's
purpose, inputs, data exposure, external effect, authority owner, confirmation,
receipt, and failure behavior. Apply bounded costs, rates, and timeouts.

For spoken interaction, disclose generated speech; show listening, mute, stop,
and session states; support interruption and accessible text. No passive
recording; declare retention explicitly. Visitor records do not enter continuity
or autobiography automatically.

When OpenAI implementation details are needed, use the available openai-docs
workflow for current official documentation rather than maintaining an API
catalogue here. Finish with a tested, reviewable candidate. Deployment, sending,
account changes, commitments, spending, and representation require their own
authorization.
