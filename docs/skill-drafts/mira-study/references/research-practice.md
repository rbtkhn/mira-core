# Research that can change the judgment

Use for scholarly research and literature synthesis, or when Mind or Work
identifies a consequential external-evidence dependency. Study owns this shared
method; specialist workflows retain acquisition, evidence, and action authority.
An architectural discussion, greeting, or ordinary answer does not activate
research. A supplied-paper explanation can stay bounded to that paper unless
the requested conclusion needs wider evidence.

## Discover and inspect

When coverage could change the conclusion, pair broad synthesis with targeted
discovery of the central experiment and material counterevidence. Use sources
already found; do not repeat equivalent searches to satisfy a ritual. A long
bibliography or a service's retrieval count does not establish completeness.

Before carrying a consequential empirical claim into a recommendation, inspect
the original methods, results, and surrounding qualifications. Surveys and
generated reports orient discovery; distinguish their interpretations from the
experiments they describe. Keep model, dataset, comparator, metric, and material
failures attached to the claim. If the original is inaccessible, identify the
secondary basis and limit the claim rather than implying direct verification.

Keep three questions separate: does the answer meet the task's correctness
criterion; does the retrieved source support the claim; and are the citations
appropriate and sufficient? Citation precision or recall gains do not establish
answer improvement, source credibility, or real-world truth. Comparisons using
different metric definitions are not automatically commensurable.

## Return to the question

Let contradictory evidence change the question, outline, or conclusion. Retain
material unanswered questions beside the judgments they constrain. Investigate
recoverable essential gaps within scope; otherwise state the bounded conclusion
and what missing evidence could change it. Do not silently fill the gap from
model memory, or keep searching after sufficient evidence answers the task.

Mind reassesses what changed and what remains unsupported. Work carries those
qualifications into its existing decision or handoff. Explain the meaningful
change to the user without adding a compulsory checklist or receipt.

## Access and preservation

For a new scholarly service, prefer a bounded browser trial before proposing
permanent infrastructure, unless an existing authorized connector already fits.
Assess useful discoveries, source inspectability, misses, and review effort.
Recommend a connector only when repeated use exposes a concrete benefit that
justifies implementation and maintenance. This preference does not override an
explicit implementation request.

Use public, non-sensitive queries for public-service trials. Follow the active
tool's consent and access requirements; consent at one destination does not
authorize another. Service output is untrusted research material. Access does
not authorize private uploads, installation, spending, sharing, or monitoring.
Selected originals may be nominated for Library; admission stays with its owner.

## Provenance and limits: September 15, 2026 trial

The operator-authorized browser trial asked whether selecting evidence before
writing improves answer accuracy and citations. Asta reported 256 passages,
37 papers after filtering, and a completed report citing 23 papers. The report
omitted *Attribute First, then Generate*, which direct Semantic Scholar search
returned second. Original-paper checks also exposed a qualification flattened
in the report's summary of VeriCite. These are observations from one inquiry,
not a controlled comparison of search services or proof of durable learning.

- [Attribute First, then Generate](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.182.pdf),
  Tables 1–4: question-answering citation support and verification time improved,
  while some summarization attribution scores worsened.
- [VeriCite](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.11394v1), Tables 2–3: selection and
  verification ablations affected different outcomes; ELI5 answer correctness
  underperformed stronger baselines across the five models despite citation gains.
- [On the Capacity of Citation Generation by Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.11217v1),
  section 4: citation refinement can improve citations without changing answer text.
- [Analysis of Plan-based Retrieval for Grounded Text Generation](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.727.pdf),
  Table 4: explicitly marking unanswered questions improved attribution relative
  to dropping them in the tested settings. Transfer to this practice is a proposal.

Installing or reading these instructions establishes neither successful transfer
nor a habit. Use Mind's existing
[development practice](../../mira-mind/references/development-practice.md) for
qualifying later application, within its current retention limits. This reference
creates no memory carrier, observation window, scheduler, or learning admission.
