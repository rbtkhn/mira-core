# Exercise record

Use this as a compact preparation sheet and provenance appendix in the existing
Mira Notes experiment folder. Omit empty machinery from the scene itself.
Unknown information stays explicitly unknown. This is not an admission schema.

For a linked follow-up, give the predecessor reference and describe the delta:
new question or constraint, changed interpretation, counterargument, and next
test. Point to unchanged source bindings and boundaries rather than copying
their full explanation. Keep any new or changed evidence gap explicit. Preserve
the exact local status and the distinct stage statuses below; compression must
not hide a missing stage or imply that inherited evidence was reverified.

## Identity and question

- Stable exercise ID and version; actual composition date; retrospective dates
  and predecessor versions separately.
- Status and exact local paths; privacy; source-work reference; no registry
  relationship, graph edge, source admission, or canonical-store authority.
  Library Integration is required for subsequent governed attachment.
- Reasoning practice, current question, relevant prior note/thread and correction;
  what retrieval changed; proposed improvement and rejection condition.
- Scene bounds, initial facts, participant knowledge, intervention being varied,
  and fixed-decision or open-counterfactual policy. Record author knowledge of
  prior results and hypotheses; never describe such a comparison as blinded.

## Interpretation and continuation

Write free prose on the best discovery, serious objection, and what survived or
failed. Compare reasoning, decision, and downstream outcome separately. For a
metaphor identify correspondence, insight, and the point where it breaks.

Name one next test that could reject the proposed practice. List each Recursive
Learn stage separately with its actual evidence or absence. Keep a compositional
check separate from later use; this appendix does not assess or admit learning.
Link an eligible Library Journal entry if one exists, not an invented placeholder.

State whether the authored artifact is complete and whether shared interpretation
is still open. These are separate judgments. Do not invent operator contributions
to close the second. Deliver the scene before the administrative afterword;
record an unresolved interpretive question without requiring another task menu.

An essay seed states a thesis, counterargument, intended reader, and what further
evidence or composition it needs. A completed essay links back to this exercise
and reports its own privacy and persistence. Preserve the source record here;
do not turn an essay's success into retroactive experimental evidence.
