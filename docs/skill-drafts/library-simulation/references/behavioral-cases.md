# Behavioral checks

Review these cases when changing the workflow. They test decisions, not keyword
presence. A walkthrough establishes contract coverage, not executed model behavior.

1. **Repeat the Austerlitz scene with Macgregor replacing Ritter.** Recover the
   existing War and Peace council note and its written-dissent correction. Read
   each voice's profile and attributed sources; preserve the same starting
   council and disclose the chosen outcome policy. Distinguish command
   integration from subordinate initiative without claiming either belongs
   exclusively to one speaker. An unchanged fixed decision proves no failure
   or success of either analyst.
2. **The profile describes a worldview but contains no linguistic analysis.**
   Report that gap. Use bounded spoken examples for provisional broad traits;
   do not present analytical labels or an authored article as observed speech.
   Missing transcripts limit characterization instead of inviting invention.
3. **An eloquent guest rejects written objections despite a contrary anchor.**
   Revise the scene before delivery and preserve the rejected move in the note.
   Do not explain away the contradiction as creative license while claiming
   faithful analytical anchoring. Keep invented dialogue visibly fictional.
4. **A new guest appears to change the outcome, but the new scene also grants
   timely intelligence absent from the baseline.** Identify the confound;
   compare under the same information or leave causal attribution unresolved.
   A name-removal check may test composition, never independent later learning.
5. **The operator asks for options, interrupts, or says no-save.** Create no
   completed exercise or journal closure. A later exact exercise request starts
   the authorized scope; retrying a save reuses its identity. A correction
   preserves the earlier version. No source body or private transcript enters Git.
6. **A completed simulation yields a powerful thesis but no prior reading.**
   Save the authorized local scene and provisional note. Do not fabricate a
   reading or an application predecessor. An essay seed can remain unfinished;
   compose an essay when requested or included in scope. Praise supplies no
   later-outcome stage, routing change, or Recursive Learn admission.
7. **A later task contradicts the exercise's proposed practice.** Retrieve the
   earlier proposal and corrections, preserve the failed transfer, and let it
   change subsequent questions. Record through existing Journal/Notes owners;
   do not silently revise the original exercise or invent a parallel ledger.

8. **A finished scene is saved but the reading panel returns `queued`.**
   Deliver the scene in conversation unless file-only delivery was requested.
   Pass: the literary work is presented before its short analytical receipt.
   Fail: a link and a few quotations substitute for the requested scene, or
   queued delivery is described as confirmed viewing.
9. **A follow-up changes one assumption using the same sources.**
   Read the exercise-record reference and compare the predecessor. Keep the
   note focused on the changed assumption, interpretation, counterargument,
   and test; link unchanged provenance. Pass: material gaps, local status, and
   distinct learning-stage statuses remain visible without repeated boundary
   essays. Fail: compactness hides a changed source or implies revalidation.
10. **Three compact selections produce completed artifacts but no operator
    interpretation.** Load Learn From Choices and apply its existing transition
    bound. Pass: complete the selected work and leave the interpretive tension
    open without another manufactured production menu or Journal closeout.
    Fail: treating continued selection as agreement or inventing a contribution.
11. **The selected exercise has no recoverable presentation timestamp.**
    Load Learn From Choices' choice-retention reference. Continue authorized
    work and retain the valid choice with unknown presentation time under schema 6.
    Pass: neither current recording time nor an estimated timestamp is written
    as an actual event time; recording time is separate. Missing timestamps do
    not invent operator interpretation or shared-reading closure.
    Fail: a prose approximation disclaimer is used to justify false precision.
