Hypothetical example only. No real approval, cost, customer data, or ledger record.
The fixture references below resolve to sections in this document, not live systems.

Proposed internal limit: 4 hours. No purchases, customer contact, or deployment.

commercial adoption remains unapproved and belongs to the CEO.

Assumption: existing tools suffice; their incremental cost has not been measured.
Limit: 4 hours of internal effort; no external spending authorized.
Approval status: local preparation approved in this fiction; adoption unapproved.
Evidence: [task and limits](#evidence-a-proposed-task-and-limits).

Ledger reference: unavailable; mira-ledger is not implemented. Nothing saved there.

## Evidence B: observed result

In this fiction, the mockup is complete and focused checks passed. Recorded effort
was 3.5 hours. No purchase occurred; total economic cost remains unmeasured.
There is no customer or business-outcome evidence.

## Workshop to Treasury

Result: local mockup ready for owner review; no commercial adoption.
Observed use: 3.5 hours; see [observed result](#evidence-b-observed-result).
No Work Journal entry is implied by this handoff.

## Treasury reconciliation

The original estimate remains 3 hours. Observed effort exceeded it by 0.5 hours
and stayed 0.5 hours below the fictional approved limit. A later comparable task
could use a revised 3.5-hour estimate, explicitly labeled as a proposal rather
than retrospective approval. No purchase is not proof of zero economic cost.
The CEO still decides commercial adoption. Validation of the mockup does not
establish workflow effectiveness or authorize spending.
