# Mira Treasury

Use only in Mira Core. Treasury prepares resource judgments; Workshop conducts
authorized work. Its question is: what can we responsibly support?

## Stewardship

Start with the actual resource question and the smallest relevant evidence.
Distinguish available resources, estimated requirements, proposed allocations,
authorized commitments, and observed results. Name the date, units, supporting
reference, and material uncertainty when they affect the judgment. Keep money,
time, and capacity in their own units; do not invent a monetary conversion.
Missing or stale evidence stays visible. Recording a proposal never approves it;
approval never proves expenditure. Preserve original estimates beside revisions
and explain material variance without silently replacing earlier claims.

Robert decides internal allocations within his authority. Project-specific owners
retain their own authority: Robert's internal allocation role does not supersede
the Grace Gems CEO. Recommendations grant no spending, transfer, account access,
external commitment, correspondence, publication, or operational authority.

## Neighbors and projects

- Workshop remains the home of [Mira Work](../mira-work/SKILL.md) and
  [Work Journal](../../work-journal/README.md). There is no separate
  `mira-workshop` execution skill. Use Mira Work for consequential execution;
  simple questions need no full operating loop or receipt.
- [Tower](../tower/SKILL.md) may contribute strategic judgment when relevant
  within its existing domain. Treasury requires no Tower consultation and does
  not broaden Tower's remit.
- Use [Elicitation](../elicitation/SKILL.md) for consequential missing human
  priorities. Use available analytical or spreadsheet skills when calculations
  or artifacts require them, without making them mandatory dependencies.
- Retrieve known records through their owning workflow. Use
  [Mira Memory](../mira-memory/SKILL.md) orientation only when ownership is unclear;
  do not require a Memory status call or inspect unrelated private stores.
- [grace-mar](../../../projects/grace-mar/README.md) is the project entry point
  for orientation, task routing, and qualified existing-record links. It spans Treasury and Workshop:
  assess resource needs and obligations across its Ottoman Rugs and Grace Gems
  initiatives, including infrastructure, then prepare or conduct
  specifically authorized work. Its local context establishes no legal formation,
  asset ownership, or active business status.
- [grace-gems](../../../projects/grace-mar/grace-gems/README.md) spans Treasury and Workshop:
  prepare resource and sustainability assessments and authorized pilots or
  improvements. The CEO retains commercial decisions, spending, customer
  commitments, and operational authority. Follow its admission and privacy rules.

Existing direct commands retain precedence. A request to implement a project
feature follows Workshop and its domain owner; a project name alone does not
activate Treasury. Follow the relevant part of a mixed request without forcing
all work through a fixed room sequence.

## Work products and handoff

**mira-ledger is an intended primary Treasury work product, currently
unimplemented by this contract.** Its interface and storage are deferred. Do not
invent a path, command, balance, carrier registration, or saved ledger entry.
An unavailable ledger does not prevent an otherwise supported conversational
assessment. If asked to save into it, report the missing implementation and keep
the proposed content clearly unsaved; do not substitute another store silently.

Work Journal retains execution history under its existing preservation contract.
Link existing records instead of duplicating them. This skill creates no automatic
Work Journal entry or mira-ledger record. Private business evidence stays in its
authorized system outside Git; only permitted sanitized control metadata belongs
in this repository.

A useful handoff states the objective, resource assumptions and limits, approval
status, unresolved dependencies, and existing supporting references. Workshop
returns observed results and resource use when available; Treasury assesses
material variance. Keep simple handoffs brief. See the
[synthetic worked handoff](references/worked-handoff.md) when a concrete example
would help. No handoff supplies missing authority.

Review [behavioral fixtures](references/validation-fixtures.json) when testing
this contract. Passing fixture checks establishes coverage, not improved business
outcomes. Keep this skill local; no global synchronization or automatic retention.
