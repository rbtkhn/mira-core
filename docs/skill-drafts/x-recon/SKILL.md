# X Recon

Use X as a public-signal and source-lead surface for Narrative Geopolitics work.
X can help discover emerging claims, official statements, analyst threads,
local-language leads, source-chain origins, and contradiction signals. It does
not verify claims by itself.

## Boundary

X reconnaissance is read-only unless the operator gives explicit bounded
approval for a specific social action. Routine geopolitics work may inspect
visible public or logged-in page state needed for the bounded task, but must
not inspect credentials, cookies, local storage, account settings, private
messages, private lists, non-task notifications, or unrelated account content.

Do not post, reply, quote, repost, like, bookmark, follow, unfollow, subscribe,
message, upload, change settings, or transmit sensitive data without exact
operator authorization for that action.

Treat X results as:

- `source-lead`: a pointer to inspect elsewhere;
- `x-reported-unverified`: a claim reported on X but not independently
  established;
- `contradiction-lead`: a denial, rival account, or contrary evidence
  candidate;
- `provenance-lead`: a possible original post, document, video, statement, or
  dataset behind a circulating claim.

Never treat likes, impressions, virality, thread confidence, blue checks, or
repeated reposts as verification.

## Best Uses

1. Forecast hook monitoring: find public signals relevant to active forecast
   hooks, especially official statements, market commentary, energy or shipping
   observations, and regional diplomatic language.
2. Contradiction hunting: search for credible denial, correction, counterdata,
   or rival source chains for a source assertion.
3. Provenance recovery: trace a circulating claim back toward its first visible
   post, linked document, official release, video, or dataset.
4. Voice accountability: identify whether an analyst revised, doubled down,
   clarified, or abandoned a position after events developed.
5. Local-language leads: surface Turkish, Persian, Arabic, Hebrew, Russian,
   Ukrainian, Chinese, or Gulf-source candidates for later verification.

## Minimal Receipt

For any useful lead, preserve:

- run date and operator-authorized scope;
- search query, account, list, or visible page inspected;
- direct X URL and any off-platform URLs linked from it;
- visible author, handle, publication date/time, and language;
- claim or signal as faithful paraphrase, not repository fact;
- source type: official, journalist, analyst, eyewitness, aggregator,
  commercial data, media outlet, or unknown;
- lineage note: original, reposted, quoted, downstream, unclear, or
  source-recovery-pending;
- reliability warning or contradiction if visible;
- next route: `reality-check`, `archive-intake`, `geo-strategy`,
  `grok-research`, `voice-revision-audit`, or backlog.

## Routing Rules

- Official statement or document lead -> inspect original source, then
  `reality-check` if claim use matters.
- Transcript, long-form video, article, or report lead -> `archive-intake` only
  after source body and metadata are available.
- Forecast signal lead -> `geo-strategy` only as an observable candidate until
  independent source review is complete.
- Grok prompt material -> `grok-research` with direct URLs and the same
  unverified status.
- Voice revision candidate -> `voice-revision-audit`; do not decide whether the
  revised claim is true inside X recon.

## Stop Conditions

Stop X reconnaissance when:

- the bounded hook or claim has three credible independent leads;
- two consecutive search passes only return reposts or derivative summaries;
- the visible page requires private messages, credentials, sensitive account
  state, or non-task personal content;
- a useful lead now requires verification rather than more X searching.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every saved X lead is labeled `source-lead`, `x-reported-unverified`,
  `contradiction-lead`, or `provenance-lead`.
- No X result is promoted to verified fact without downstream adjudication.
- No social action or sensitive-data transmission occurs without explicit
  bounded operator approval.
- The final handoff names the next governed route rather than turning X recon
  into synthesis.
