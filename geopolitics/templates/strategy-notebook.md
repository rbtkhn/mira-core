Register guidance:

- `expert-room`: concise, source-bound, decision-relevant. Prioritize analytic
  usability, source pressure, held claims, and indicators.
- `strategic-memorandum`: historically conscious, more architectural, and
  sparingly literary. Use only when the packet reveals an order-level mechanism

  architecture, historical recurrence, or legitimacy fracture.

uncertainty, and restraint. Do not use inward journal language, ceremonial
cadence, newsletter framing, self-formation language, or lyrical reflection.

source inventory, synthesis, judgment, forecast ledger, issue rendering, or
Reality verification. It is not public copy, policy advice, operational
guidance, or proof of any source claim.
