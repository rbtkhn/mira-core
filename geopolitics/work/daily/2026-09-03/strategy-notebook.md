## Strategic Question

Can Washington sustain partner confidence across the Gulf and Europe when
partners' exposure increases faster than their confidence in protection, and
what evidence would distinguish durable realignment from temporary bargaining?

## Bottom Line

The useful continuity from September 2 is pressure on the systems that carry
coercion. September 3 gives that mechanism a more specific political question:
whether protection commitments still organize partner choices when the
protecting power is itself implicated in the exposure. The Gulf interview lanes
describe host vulnerability, economic retaliation and alternative security
arrangements; the European lanes describe pressure to draw Washington back
into confrontation. These are contrasting possible responses to dependence,
not evidence of one coordinated cross-theater process.

Confidence: `low`

intentions. Repeated Mercouris appearances and shared interview hosts also

reciprocal settlement could weaken the mechanism without eliminating hostile
