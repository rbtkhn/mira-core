## Lead Judgment

The August 27 corpus does not show a clean coercive breakthrough by the United States or its partners. It shows a pressure system becoming harder to manage across linked theaters. The Russia-Ukraine lane emphasizes battlefield and air-defense exhaustion, while the Iran lane emphasizes sanctions, base exposure, and regional retaliation risk. The shared mechanism is margin compression: diplomatic visits, sanctions threats, carrier movement, and allied escalation talk still matter, but they increasingly function as burden-management signals rather than decisive instruments.
