## Load-Bearing Judgments

1. Access and conversion constrain usable capacity but do not specify an attainable political objective. Their binding force must be observed, not inferred from resource counts or claims of damage.
2. Limited objectives and adaptation are the strongest rival to inevitable exhaustion. Defensive protection, signaling and costly continuing operations may satisfy narrower aims than conquest or unconditional surrender.
3. Settlement requires authority over promised behavior, including partners. Envoy access, pressure and rhetoric cannot substitute for reciprocal implementation.
4. The ten-source set sharpens these distinctions but has too little independent operational evidence to establish destroyer disablement, universal fuel shortage or predetermined political collapse.

## Confidence Boundary

Confidence: `medium`
