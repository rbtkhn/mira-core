## Draft Judgment

The batch supports a bounded judgment: infrastructure and access are not background details; they are the mechanism through which escalation becomes politically and commercially costly. The Iran/Hormuz cluster is strongest as a convergence of commentator reasoning, while the exact private threats, corridor terms, and attribution remain provisional. Mercouris’s Odessa comparison adds a useful limit: maritime access can become functionally intolerable before a theater is fully sealed, but that claim also requires independent shipping evidence.
