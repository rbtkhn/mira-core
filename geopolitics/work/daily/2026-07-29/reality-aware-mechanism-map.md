## Synthesis Gain

The new distinction is between **carrier evidence** and **control claims**. The transcripts repeatedly describe Israel or Netanyahu as a carrier of escalation—through access, intelligence, regional positioning, and political timing. The lattice can test the concrete carriers one by one, but it cannot infer personal control of Trump from a chain of attributed incidents.

Macgregor: “Israel has us under control and is committing us, our resources, our interests, our armed forces.” That is the day's most explicit control thesis, but it remains a speaker claim. The more durable working judgment is narrower: **the US may be materially enabling an Israeli-preferred escalation sequence while losing the ability to control its downstream tempo.**
