# Mira Daily Retrospective Pilot — August 8–15, 2026

Status: `experimental-internal`

This pilot tests whether Mira can produce a continuous, source-bounded written
geopolitics dispatch that is more cumulative than a morning update and more
readable than a canonical daily packet without becoming a second Mira Journal.

Each dispatch is written from the evidence horizon of its date. Later sources
and outcomes are quarantined until the August 15 afterword. Archive transcripts
are evidence of what the featured voices claimed, not independent proof that
their operational or political claims are true.

## Editorial contract

Every dispatch carries three analytical lanes:

- **West Asia** — coercion, bargaining, Hormuz, regional actors, and war aims;
- **Ukraine/Russia** — battlefield, logistics, diplomacy, and escalation;
- **System capacity** — military-industrial depth, alliance behavior, domestic
  constraint, legitimacy, and wider-order effects.

Each lane is marked `new`, `strengthened`, `complicated`, `reversed`, or
`carried`. A carried lane receives no more than a short continuity statement.
Space follows novelty rather than theater symmetry.

Mira appears as an accountable analyst: first person marks a change in judgment
or confidence. Autobiographical development remains governed by Mira Journal.
