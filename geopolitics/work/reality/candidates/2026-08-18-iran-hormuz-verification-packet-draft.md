## Issue Object

Iran-Hormuz pressure as a political-economic coercion campaign against Trump
before the 2026 midterms.

## Bounded Working Claim

External multilingual sources support the observable substrate: constrained
Hormuz traffic, U.S. blockade enforcement, Oman-Iran navigation talks,
competing legal/sovereignty narratives, oil/refined-product stress, and Trump's
escalatory Oman/Hormuz rhetoric.

The stronger claim that Iran is deliberately converting Hormuz pressure into a
U.S. midterm coercion strategy remains a plausible strategic interpretation,
not an externally verified outcome.

## Mechanism Spine

`Hormuz access dispute -> selective/conditional passage -> energy and refined-product stress -> U.S. domestic confidence decline -> midterm/congressional constraint -> Trump escalation dilemma`
