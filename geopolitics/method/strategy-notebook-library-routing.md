# Strategy Notebook Library Routing

Status: `draft`

Purpose: route Mira Library into Strategy Notebook composition only when a
historical pressure test can improve the estimate without becoming evidence for
present facts.

## Invocation Threshold

Invoke Library only after the Geo source set has produced a provisional crisis
object and mechanism. At least one gate must be true:

- the notebook uses a named historical analogy or anti-analogy;
- the mechanism depends on a recurring statecraft pattern that Library can
  test, such as maritime order, indirect coercion, commerce becoming
  sovereignty, alliance burden, logistics as strategy, settlement architecture,
  legitimacy under overreach, or frontier governance;
- the intended reader needs historical framing to use the estimate responsibly;
- a metadata pre-scan returns at least one registered `LIB-*` family with
  available text and a plausible analytic job;
- the estimate's confidence would change if Library exposed an anachronism,
  rival mechanism, structural difference, or missing rejection condition.

If no gate fires, record Library as `not-invoked` or omit the section.

## Routing Registry

| Mechanism Signature | Candidate Handles | Analytic Job | Anti-Analogy Warning | Rejection Condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `maritime_access_order` | Grotius; UN Charter; naval and treaty corpora | Test whether passage is treated as legal order, imperial assurance, or practical control. | Formal sea-law language can hide coercive facts and commercial risk. | Reject when the present claim depends on unverified operational control rather than order language. |
| `coercion_indirect_instruments` | Kautilya; sanctions and administrative texts; frontier statecraft | Test whether pressure works through carriers, clients, finance, routes, and intermediaries. | Ancient or imperial counsel can overstate intentional design in modern bureaucratic systems. | Reject when the present source set shows only destructive capacity, not arranged choices. |
| `commerce_to_sovereignty` | East India Company; VOC; charter and revenue materials | Test when commercial access becomes governing authority. | Company rule analogies can smuggle colonial structure into unlike sovereign settings. | Reject when commerce remains a market effect rather than institutionalized rule. |
| `alliance_burden` | Federalist Papers; UN Charter; coalition diplomacy texts | Test whether allied consent, burden, or legitimacy constrains coercion. | Institutional texts may describe ideals more than operating stress. | Reject when allied burden does not alter the actor's available choices. |
| `logistics_as_strategy` | Caesar; Tacitus; campaign and frontier accounts | Test whether routes, supplies, bases, and winter endurance carry the political strategy. | Frontier campaign analogies can flatten industrial and technological differences. | Reject when logistics are background conditions rather than the contested object. |
| `settlement_architecture` | Westphalia; UN Charter; treaty corpora; imperial administrative settlements | Test whether coercion has produced, blocked, or outrun a political arrangement. | Settlement analogies can make absence look like design. | Reject when no actor is attempting an arrangement that can be described. |
| `legitimacy_under_overreach` | Thucydidean/classical, anti-colonial, and reform texts where registered | Test whether force creates resistance, exposure, or legitimacy loss. | Moral resemblance is not structural identity. | Reject when legitimacy language does not change coercive capacity or choices. |

## Notebook Use

Library material may enter the Strategy Notebook only as an adjudicated pressure
test. Use `adopted`, `narrowed`, `redirected`, `rejected`, `held`, or
`not-invoked`.

Every adopted, narrowed, or redirected row must state:

- the shared mechanism;
- the decisive structural difference;
- the rejection condition;
- the effect on the estimate.

## Boundary

`LIB-*` references do not satisfy `SRC-*` coverage, verify `OPC-*` or `NG-*`
claims, resolve forecasts, establish numerical base rates, or authorize public
promotion. Library absence remains absence.
