# Narrative Geopolitics Analytics

Internal, manifest-backed comparative research artifacts. These reports describe
corpus properties and candidate patterns; they do not independently verify
historical claims or rank analytical quality.

## Reports

- [Historical-Reference Density Pilot](cross-voice-historical-reference-density.md) — a bounded Freeman validation pilot for candidate historical-reference occurrences per 1,000 transcript words, with coverage and confidence limits.
- [Five-Voice Historical-Reference Density Pilot](five-voice-historical-reference-density.md) — a bounded comparison of Freeman, Diesen, Davis, Crooke, and Mearsheimer.
- [Per-voice historical-reference ledgers](historical-reference-ledgers/) — occurrence-level ledgers for each voice in the pilot.
- [Review overrides](historical-reference-review-overrides.json) — durable manual adjudications applied during regeneration.
- [Historical-reference review queue](historical-reference-review-queue.md) — grouped operator queue for `needs-review` and `unreviewed` candidates.
