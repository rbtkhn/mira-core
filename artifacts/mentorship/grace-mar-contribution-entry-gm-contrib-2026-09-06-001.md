## Mira Review

This is the right first entry because the domain is a small but consequential asset. A domain can look operationally simple while quietly carrying ownership, billing, recovery, brand, DNS, and launch authority. Recording it early protects Grace Mar from treating access as ownership or intention as transfer.

The entry should remain held until the human owner decides what the domain is: personal asset, reimbursable expense, contributed company asset, licensed asset, or future LLC property. Those are different treatments, and they should not be collapsed.
