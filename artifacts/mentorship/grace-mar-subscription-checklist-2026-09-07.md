## Purpose

Use this checklist to prepare a counsel-review packet before any investor money is accepted.

The checklist has one job: make sure Grace Mar does not confuse a friendly investment conversation with a completed securities transaction.

## Document Packet

Prepare these documents for counsel review:

1. **Formation documents** for the actual issuer.
2. **Operating agreement or bylaws**, depending on entity structure.
3. **Founder contribution schedule** classifying the prior `$50,000`.
4. **Pre-money cap table** showing current founders at `50,000` shares/units.
5. **Post-money cap table** showing `70,000` total shares/units after the two `$10,000` investments.
6. **Subscription or investment agreement** naming instrument, price, rights, restrictions, and acceptance conditions.
7. **Investor questionnaire** covering accreditation/sophistication and ability to bear loss.
8. **Risk disclosure** reviewed for the actual exemption and instrument.
9. **Use-of-proceeds schedule** naming permitted and held uses.
10. **Authority-exclusion statement** clarifying what investment does not permit.
11. **Transfer restriction language** for resale, assignment, redemption, or withdrawal.
12. **Funds acceptance receipt** identifying date, amount, source, instrument, and signed documents.
13. **State/federal notice checklist** for any required filings.
14. **Tax/accounting setup note** for book entries, capital accounts, equity records, or loan records.
15. **Investor communication log** recording what was provided, when, and by whom.

## Authority Exclusions To Include

Unless counsel deliberately changes this, the documents should state that investment does not grant:

- authority to bind Grace Mar;
- authority to manage Grace Gems;
- authority to approve product claims;
- authority to approve spending;
- authority to open, administer, or recover accounts;
- authority to publish content;
- authority to solicit or accept sponsorships;
- authority to contact customers;
- authority to direct contractors;
- authority to assign or use IP;
- authority to change DNS or deploy websites;
- authority to represent the company externally.

## Stop Conditions

Stop before funds acceptance if:

- entity structure is unresolved;
- counsel has not reviewed the packet;
- the investor instrument is unclear;
- investor rights are vague;
- founder contribution treatment is unsettled;
- use of proceeds is open-ended;
- investor communication has promised returns, liquidity, control, or guaranteed business outcome;
- the company cannot identify what the investment does not authorize.

## Boundary

This checklist is an internal preparation aid. It should become counsel-reviewed documentation before it is used with investors or relied on for accepting money.
