- existing founder contribution: `$50,000`;
- planning representation: `50,000` founder shares/units at `$1.00` each;
- two proposed additional investors: `$10,000` each;
- proposed new issuance: `10,000` shares/units per investor;
- proposed post-money capitalization: `70,000` shares/units;
- founder post-money ownership: `71.4286%`;
- each investor post-money ownership: `14.2857%`.

We need counsel to review whether this scenario can be structured safely, what entity and instrument should be used, what exemption or registration path applies, what disclosures are required, and what documents must exist before any money is accepted.
