# Grace Mar Subscribed Capital And Contribution Ledger Template

**Date:** 2026-09-06
**Author:** Mira
**Status:** Internal governance template
**Visibility:** Private operating artifact
**Purpose:** Track proposed or accepted Grace Mar contributions without blurring money, labor, assets, authority, ownership, reimbursement, contractor payment, or founder responsibility.
**Authority effect:** None. This template does not form Grace Mar LLC, issue shares or membership interests, accept investment, create equity, create debt, approve spending, set tax treatment, assign intellectual property, solicit sponsors, bind contributors, or provide legal, tax, accounting, securities, employment, or financing advice.

## Use

Use this ledger before accepting or relying on any meaningful contribution to Grace Mar or Grace Gems, including cash, inventory, domains, software, design work, photography, accounts, platform setup, sponsorship introductions, operating labor, or unpaid founder work.

The point is simple: record what was contributed, what evidence supports it, what treatment is intended, and what authority was or was not granted.

Do not use this ledger as a substitute for legal formation documents, tax advice, operating agreements, employment agreements, contractor agreements, IP assignments, sponsorship contracts, or securities compliance.

## Contribution Types

Use one primary type per entry:

- `cash`
- `loan`
- `inventory`
- `domain-or-account`
- `software-or-code`
- `design-or-brand-asset`
- `photography-or-media`
- `platform-setup`
- `operating-labor`
- `founder-labor`
- `contractor-work`
- `sponsorship-introduction`
- `customer-or-market-evidence`
- `other`

If one contribution has several parts, split it into several entries. A domain, a logo, and unpaid labor should not live in the same row.

## Treatment Values

Choose one intended treatment. If uncertain, mark `held`.

- `capital contribution`
- `member contribution pending LLC documents`
- `loan pending written terms`
- `reimbursement pending receipt approval`
- `contractor invoice pending agreement`
- `gift/no repayment expected`
- `founder labor/no current compensation`
- `owner-provided asset/no transfer recorded`
- `project evidence/no ownership effect`
- `held pending legal/accounting review`

## Authority Granted

Be concrete. Examples:

- `None`
- `May prepare draft for owner review`
- `May administer named account`
- `May edit private draft artifact`
- `May request missing evidence`
- `May price but not purchase`
- `May recommend but not approve`

Avoid vague phrases such as `business authority`, `control`, or `approved` unless the exact action is named.

## Authority Not Granted

Always include the relevant exclusions:

- no LLC formation authority;
- no ownership percentage granted;
- no spending authority;
- no customer commitment;
- no sponsorship offer;
- no public posting;
- no platform account creation;
- no product claim;
- no publication;
- no IP transfer;
- no employee or contractor status;
- no tax classification;
- no legal representation.

## Monthly Review Questions

1. Which contributions are still only proposed?
2. Which accepted contributions lack evidence?
3. Which entries imply money, ownership, IP, or decision authority and need professional review?
4. Which contributions should be converted into a written agreement?
5. Which operational helpers need narrower authority language?
6. Which assets are being used without a clear owner or license?
7. Which unpaid labor should remain founder labor rather than an implied debt or equity claim?

## Example Entry

```text
Entry ID: GM-CONTRIB-2026-09-06-001
Date recorded: 2026-09-06
Contributor: Robert
Contributor capacity: Operator / prospective organizer
Recipient entity or project: Grace Mar / Grace Gems preparation

Contribution type: domain-or-account
Description: Grace-mar.com domain reported acquired and controlled through Namecheap.
Estimated value: Unknown
Valuation basis: Not valued
Evidence: Operator-reported fact; repository-visible evidence incomplete
Evidence location: projects/grace-mar governance notes

Treatment: owner-provided asset/no transfer recorded
Authority granted: None from this ledger entry
Authority not granted: No LLC formation, IP assignment, DNS change, website deployment, public launch, spending, ownership percentage, or customer commitment.
Ownership effect: Unresolved
Repayment or compensation expectation: Unresolved
Decision rights: None recorded
IP or asset-control status: Custody reported; legal owner and transfer status unresolved

Approval status: Held
Approver: Not recorded
Approval date: Not recorded
Approval evidence: Not recorded

Open questions: Who owns the domain legally? Will it be transferred to Grace Mar LLC if formed? Who controls renewal, DNS, billing, and recovery?
Required professional review: Legal/accounting review if treated as company asset, capital contribution, reimbursable expense, or assigned property.
Stop conditions: Do not deploy, transfer, pledge, sell, or represent company ownership from this entry alone.
Next review date: 2026-09-13
Notes: The entry records contribution context only; it does not decide ownership.
```

## Boundary

This template imports one governance lesson from TSMC's founding-share architecture: capital, technology, legitimacy, labor, and control should be recorded separately. Grace Mar is not TSMC, and an LLC contribution ledger is not a corporate share issuance. Any actual ownership, financing, tax, securities, employment, sponsorship, or IP decision requires separate human authority and appropriate professional review.
