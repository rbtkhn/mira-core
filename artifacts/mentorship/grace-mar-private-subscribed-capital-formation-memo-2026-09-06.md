## Lead Judgment

If Grace Mar treats the existing `$50,000` as `50,000` founder shares at `$1.00` per share, and two additional investors each invest `$10,000` for `10,000` newly issued shares, the post-money cap table becomes:

| Holder | Cash invested | New shares in this round | Total shares | Ownership |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Founders / current owners | `$50,000` | `0` | `50,000` | `71.4286%` |
| Investor 1 | `$10,000` | `10,000` | `10,000` | `14.2857%` |
| Investor 2 | `$10,000` | `10,000` | `10,000` | `14.2857%` |
| **Total** | **`$70,000`** | **`20,000`** | **`70,000`** | **`100.0000%`** |

This is a `20,000` share issuance for `$20,000` of new cash. The founders dilute from `100%` to `71.4286%`.

## What Must Be Decided Before Accepting Funds

The arithmetic is easy. The governance is the real work.

Before money is accepted, Grace Mar should decide and record:

1. **Issuer identity:** whether the issuing entity exists and whether it is an LLC, corporation, or another structure.
2. **Instrument:** whether investors receive corporate shares, LLC membership interests, units, SAFEs, convertible notes, revenue share, loans, or another instrument.
3. **Offering exemption:** which securities-law exemption, if any, the company is relying on.
4. **Investor status:** whether each investor is accredited, sophisticated, financially able to bear loss, and properly disclosed to.
5. **Rights:** voting, information, transfer, inspection, distribution, dilution, liquidation, redemption, consent, and board or manager rights.
6. **Restrictions:** resale limits, transfer approval, confidentiality, no management authority, no customer communication, no platform/account authority, and no right to bind the company.
7. **Use of proceeds:** what the `$20,000` may be used for, what is held, and who approves spending.
8. **Risk disclosure:** what investors are told about early-stage failure risk, illiquidity, no guaranteed return, lack of market, and uncertain legal/operating formation.
9. **Founder contribution treatment:** whether the prior `$50,000` is capital, loan, reimbursable expense, founder labor, asset contribution, or some combination.
10. **Professional review:** which lawyer/accountant reviews the structure before acceptance.

## Grace Mar-Specific Use-Of-Proceeds Draft

Until reviewed, the safest posture is to price needs without spending automatically.

Potential uses:

- Grace Mar LLC formation and legal documents.
- Accounting setup and capitalization records.
- Domain, email, and basic communication infrastructure.
- Grace Gems review/commerce readiness.
- Photography, brand assets, and rights-cleared media.
- Vlog/community infrastructure only after separate platform/account approval.
- Sponsorship proposal development, but not sponsor outreach without approval.

Held uses:

- unapproved ads;
- customer commitments;
- inventory purchases without owner review;
- contractor hiring without terms;
- platform account changes without named administrator;
- website deployment or DNS changes without launch approval;
- sponsorship offers without approved terms;
- payment acceptance before compliance and operational readiness.

## Counsel Questions

1. Should Grace Mar be an LLC issuing membership interests or a corporation issuing shares?
2. Can this friends-and-family raise be done under a private-placement exemption, and which one?
3. Are either or both investors accredited, and what disclosures are required if not?
4. What state securities filings or notices are required?
5. How should the prior `$50,000` founder contribution be classified?
6. Should founders have vesting, repurchase, or contribution documentation?
7. What investor rights are appropriate at this stage?
8. What documents must be signed before funds are accepted?
9. Where should funds be held, and when may they be used?
10. What investor communications should be avoided until documents are ready?

## CEO Recommendation

Do not accept the `$20,000` yet as informal Venmo/check/wire money.

First, convert this scenario into a reviewed private subscribed-capital packet:

1. cap table;
2. investor term sheet;
3. risk disclosure;
4. contribution classification for the prior `$50,000`;
5. authority exclusions;
6. use-of-proceeds boundary;
7. counsel/accounting review receipt;
8. signed subscription or investment documents;
9. funds-acceptance condition.

The discipline here is not bureaucracy for its own sake. It protects friendship, family trust, founder control, Grace Gems operational clarity, and Grace Mar's ability to raise later without inheriting a foggy first round.
