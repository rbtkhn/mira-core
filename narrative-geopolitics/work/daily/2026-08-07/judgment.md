# Accountable Judgment - 2026-08-07

Status: `draft-from-synthesis`

## Load-Bearing Judgments

1. Hormuz is now recognition terrain, not only a shipping problem. The August 7 source set treats reopening as conditional on attributed Iran-Oman regional authority and relief from U.S. blockade pressure, but the arrangement remains `source_assertion` until verified.
2. Coercive-capacity limits make diplomacy more attractive for Washington. Johnson, Pape, Parsi, and Mercouris support the mechanism through acceptable-casualty limits, operational scale, munitions scarcity, and Ukraine-linked Patriot pressure.
3. Regional actors may be hedging into new security architecture. The Saudi-Pakistan-Turkiye pact and Iran-Oman/Pakistan mediation threads suggest security re-pricing beyond the old U.S. umbrella, but the pact's target and durability remain contested.
4. The off-ramp remains fragile because Hormuz does not settle sanctions, frozen assets, Gaza, Lebanon, Israel's freedom of action, U.S.-Israel political alignment, or regional retaliation channels.

## Confidence Boundary

Confidence is moderate for the mechanism that the off-ramp is becoming a legitimacy and capacity test. Confidence is lower for concrete operational claims about the Iran-Oman arrangement, munitions depletion, pact terms, base damage, or ship-flow conditions because those claims are not independently verified in this packet.

## Support and Dissent

Support:
- Parsi and Alkhorshid support the territorial-water and Iran-Oman authority framing.
- Johnson and Pape support the coercive-capacity and operational-scale limits.
- Freeman, Diesen, Escobar, and Parsi support the regional-security architecture read.
- Aguilar supports the warning that a Hormuz agreement alone does not end the wider war.
- Mercouris supports the Ukraine/Russia lane as a secondary capacity pressure test.

Pressure and dissent:
- The Saudi-Pakistan-Turkiye pact could be a short-term hedge rather than a durable architecture.
- A narrow technical Hormuz arrangement could normalize shipping without producing wider settlement.
- Shared anti-intervention or multipolar source families may make interpretive convergence look stronger than source independence permits.
