## Lead Judgment

The day's useful delta is a command problem: coercion can remain active while political control weakens. Weichert, Johnson, and Marandi describe, from different positions, maritime confrontation, reported military dissent, and an Iranian claim to a new Hormuz order. None of those operating claims is independently verified here. Together they support a bounded mechanism judgment: if military and political actors no longer agree on the purpose and endpoint of pressure, each new coercive act can make an off-ramp harder to define.
