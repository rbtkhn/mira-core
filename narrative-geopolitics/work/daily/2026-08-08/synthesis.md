## Lead Judgment

The August 8 batch is useful because it shows coercion losing its quiet baseline. The archive does not establish the reported ship boarding, private U.S. military dissent, Iranian strike claims, or Ukraine battlefield claims as operating facts. It does show several routed voices treating those claims as signs that pressure is no longer producing a clean settlement story. The mechanism is reputational: once coercion has to be repeatedly explained as limited, successful, or temporary, every new incident becomes a test of whether the coercer still controls the escalation frame.
