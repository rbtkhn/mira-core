## Draft Judgment

August 3 is best read as the point where the week's Hormuz story becomes a sequencing problem. If Washington is reported to be negotiating or conceding while Iran keeps demonstrating maritime leverage, the question becomes whether diplomacy can be narrated as controlled de-escalation rather than coerced adjustment. That matters because the later August 4-7 packets all depend on the same mechanism: a narrow transit arrangement can exist while the wider war logic remains unresolved.

The Russia/Ukraine lane belongs as a pressure test. Mercouris and Ritter reinforce the idea that Western strategy faces material and credibility limits across theaters, but the lead object remains Iran/Hormuz.
