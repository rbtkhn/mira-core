## Draft Judgment

- The operational substrate is strong enough for internal synthesis: Hormuz traffic disruption, U.S. blockade enforcement, Oman-Iran talks, and oil-price stress are externally supported.
- The strategic interpretation is useful but not verified: Iran may benefit from making Trump own fuel-price and navigation instability before the midterms, but the record does not prove that this is an official Iranian electoral plan.
- The discriminating threshold is passage governance, not closure alone. Selective or conditional route control can create political pressure while preserving bargaining room.
- The Johnson/Moral Resistance source adds a second economic-pressure object: whether alleged UAE financial restrictions matter if Iran's payment and trade routes have shifted toward China, Russia, Pakistan, and non-dollar rails. Treat this as a verification candidate, not a settled fact.
- The highest-risk overclaim is nuclear or infrastructure intent. Those belong as escalation scenarios and dissent pressure, not as present-tense verified planning.
