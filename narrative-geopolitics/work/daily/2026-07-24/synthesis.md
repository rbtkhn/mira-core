## Lead Judgment

The apparent off-ramp is becoming a mechanism of escalation rather than exit. Threats and limited strikes are being used to recover leverage, but each move increases pressure on connected maritime routes, energy exports, and regional bases. Washington can widen damage faster than it can restore a stable bargaining channel.
