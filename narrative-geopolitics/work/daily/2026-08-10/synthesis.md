## Lead Judgment

The central judgment is that exit terms have become coercive instruments. Reparations, delayed agreement, surrender language, sanctions, and regional force threats each raise the political price of compromise. Continued pressure may therefore preserve bargaining activity while reducing the set of mutually acceptable terms.

## Draft Judgment

The bargaining field is hardening because public demands do political work at home and strategic work abroad. The archive should distinguish demands used for signaling from terms that appear in verified negotiations. A narrowing gap between rhetoric and documented terms would weaken the judgment; continued escalation of preconditions would strengthen it.
