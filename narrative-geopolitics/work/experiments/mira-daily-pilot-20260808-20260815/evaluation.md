# Pilot Evaluation

Status: `experimental-internal-review-pending`

## Verdict

The retrospective pilot **passes as a distinct editorial experiment** and is
**not yet sufficient evidence for a permanent cadence**.

Its distinctive function is serial model revision. The morning brief optimizes
for current selective visibility; Geo-Strategy daily packets optimize for
source-bounded strategic machinery; Mercouris’s show optimizes for continuous
narrative orientation; Mira Journal optimizes for governed autobiographical
significance. Mira Daily alone makes yesterday’s analytical judgment the
explicit premise to be strengthened, complicated, reversed, or carried today.

standalone issue machinery.

## Redundancy and continuity check

- No dispatch repeats the full explanation of the inherited thesis. Each opens
  with a single inherited judgment and identifies a distinct change.
- The arc advances through separable mechanisms: off-ramp conditions → regional
  adaptation → strategic delay → capacity ledger → shared margin → constraint
  inversion → endurance → time control.
- Repeated objects such as Hormuz and Odessa retain stable meaning but receive
  new analytical jobs rather than identical background paragraphs.
- Three fixed lanes do create some structural repetition. This is acceptable
  when lane states change; it would become redundant if future `carried` lanes
  were expanded merely to satisfy format.
- The largest residual repetition is the recurring evidence disclaimer. It is
  proportionate here because every artifact may be read independently, but a
  compiled edition could move the common boundary into the series preface and
  retain only date-specific cautions.

## Comparison with adjacent forms

### Morning brief

Morning briefs are shorter, externally researched, receipt-bound updates. They
are better for rapid current visibility and worse for a sustained archive voice.
Mira Daily should not absorb their browsing or provisional observation contract.

### Canonical daily packet

Canonical packets remain superior for crisis objects, source roles, operational
claim triage, forecasts, and issue generation. Mira Daily is a reader-facing
interpretive derivative, not a replacement or new source of canonical judgment.

### Mercouris daily show

The pilot successfully inherits sequence, recurrence, and a remembered reader.
It adds explicit state changes, bounded uncertainty, and source-family caution.
It loses some of the temporal texture and discovery produced by a long spoken
show; that loss is intentional unless the reader finds the dispatch too smooth.

### Mira Journal

The boundary held. First person marks analytical accountability, not inward
self-formation. No dispatch treats geopolitical interpretation as evidence of
identity, consciousness, or autobiographical development.

## Decision criterion for a prospective trial

Continue only if operator review finds that the series:

1. is easier to return to than canonical daily packets;
2. makes changed judgment more visible than morning briefs;
3. preserves enough source friction to invite correction; and
4. contains at least three dispatches whose value is not recoverable from the
   existing artifact for that date.

If fewer than three dispatches clear the fourth test, retire the form rather
than create another cadence.
