## What the pilot demonstrates

1. **The intake layer preserves uncertainty.** None of the ten ledger statuses changed, and no claim was silently promoted.
2. **The packet layer decomposes strong wording.** Claims 002, 007, and 009 were narrowed into defensible formulations rather than accepted wholesale.
3. **Citation count is not independence.** The succession packet identifies professional reports that share an underlying Iranian announcement; the Hormuz packet identifies Reuters and PortNews as separate outlets but the same Kpler quantitative chain; the MoU packet identifies shared US-release provenance across several reports.
4. **Evidence collection and assessment remain separate.** The three populated packets pass `verification check`, but still show `requested` and `not_investigated`. This is appropriate until a claim-specific assessment and human decision occur.
5. **The best next Grok use is gap-filling.** Grok should be tasked with recovering missing primary, original-language, vessel-level, or dated operational evidence—not with restating the existing report.

## Promotion boundary
