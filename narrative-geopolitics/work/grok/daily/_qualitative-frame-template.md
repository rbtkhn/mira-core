## Qualitative reference frame

Ask of each voice:

- Did the voice distinguish an announcement from an implemented event?
- Did the voice distinguish a party’s claim from independently observed evidence?
- Did the voice define operational terms, denominators, and time windows?
- Did the voice identify source lineage and citation dependence?
- Did the voice separate tactical success from strategic or political effect?
- Did the voice state what evidence would change the assessment?
- Did the voice revise or narrow an earlier claim when stronger evidence appeared?

These are qualitative prompts, not scoring dimensions.
