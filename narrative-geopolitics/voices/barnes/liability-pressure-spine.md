# Robert Barnes Liability Pressure Spine

Status: `analysis-scaffold`

## Boundary

This spine summarizes Barnes's archive function for synthesis routing. It does
not verify the factual claims in Barnes sources, promote any claim to a
judgment object, or make unreviewed transcripts quote-ready.

Use source files through [source-index.md](source-index.md), then check the
host/channel shelf when `host_slug` is not `barnes`.

## Native Lens

Barnes brings the legal-political pressure map. His strongest archive function
is not battlefield confirmation or diplomatic chronology, but the liability
chain around escalation: who is exposed, who needs chaos, who cannot safely
back down, who benefits from delay, which institutional brake has failed, and
how an adversary can turn domestic political incentives into strategic leverage.

Use Barnes when a crisis looks irrational on operational grounds and the archive
needs to explain why the irrational path still has momentum.

## Use Guidance

Use Barnes for:

- domestic political incentives behind foreign-policy escalation;
- leader psychology as an operational variable;
- legal exposure, impeachment risk, prosecution risk, or institutional
  accountability;
- lobby, donor, faction, election, and media pressure on national-security
  decisions;
- explanations of why a strategically weak option keeps being selected;
- adversary leverage over humiliation avoidance, sunk cost, or deal refusal.

Do not use Barnes as:

- standalone verification of military events, casualty claims, internal
  deliberations, or operational facts;
- proof that a legal or political consequence will occur;
- public quotation support when the underlying source is marked
  `quotation_readiness: not-ready`;
- evidence that the voice has a canonical judgment unless
  [judgment-ledger.md](judgment-ledger.md) contains one.

## September 1 Iran Application

The two `2026-09-01` Barnes sources extend the same mechanism family across
Daniel Davis Deep Dive and Dialogue Works:

1. The sources claim that U.S.-Iran escalation persists because Trump cannot
   accept or honor offramps, which implies the Strait/Hormuz conflict is driven
   partly by leader psychology and domestic political image.
2. The sources claim that Iran benefits from continued kinetic conflict because
   it supplies pretext to keep the strait closed and harden its posture, which
   implies U.S. strikes may strengthen Iran's leverage rather than compel
   capitulation.
3. The sources claim that interceptor depletion is a central vulnerability
   because even intercepted attacks consume scarce defenses, which implies
   tactical interception success can still become strategic attrition.
4. The sources claim that realist-restraint personnel have been removed or
   isolated, which implies the internal U.S. decision environment is becoming
   less able to interrupt escalation.
5. The Dialogue Works source adds a wider order-shift hook: sanctions and
   maritime coercion push non-Western actors toward alternative financial rails
   and Eurasian alignment.

These are mechanism hooks for later synthesis or verification. They are not
adjudicated findings.
