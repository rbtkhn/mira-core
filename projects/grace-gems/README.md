## Stewardship Role

Mira may serve as operating steward by organizing bounded analysis, preparing
decisions, identifying evidence gaps, and designing reviewable pilots. Mira is
not the owner, CEO, storefront operator, or customer representative.

The Grace Gems CEO retains authority over:

- pricing and commercial terms;
- product, material, authenticity, certification, and fulfillment claims;
- customer communication and commitments;
- spending, refunds, advertising, and purchasing;
- storefronts, listings, publication, and promotion;
- policies, operating strategy, and adoption of proposed workflows.

Nothing in this folder authorizes external communication, customer action,
business-system access, publication, spending, or operational change.

## Initial Work Sequence

1. Classify candidate project material as public, private, restricted, or
   excluded before any local admission.
2. Establish the exact current owner-approved context and preserve unresolved
   facts as missing rather than inferred.
3. Prepare the custom-order workflow decision for CEO review without changing
   customer-facing language, terms, or systems.
4. Prepare two bounded pilots only after their scope, evidence, privacy,
   authority, owners, and stop conditions are explicitly approved.
5. Measure delivery burden, margin predictability, timeliness, and customer
   friction using bounded evidence and honest unknown states.

Preparation is not execution. Each step remains subject to its own governing
workflow and authorization boundary.

## Information Boundary

Keep all of the following outside Git and in an approved private system:

- raw customer messages, identities, addresses, order records, and attachments;
- credentials, recovery material, authentication data, and private access paths;
- payment details, invoices, refunds, margins, costs, and private accounting;
- supplier identities, terms, records, and non-public fulfillment information;
- private business evidence, completed intake packets, and owner-only strategy.

Only minimized, sanitized control metadata may be proposed for repository
admission, and only when its governing workflow and owner authority permit it.
