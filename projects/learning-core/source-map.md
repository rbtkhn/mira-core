## Interpretation and exclusions

- The upstream free-entry build offer and its separate builder-side retainer
  assertion are distinct historical statements. The retainer is explicitly
  evidence-pending in its source. Neither establishes a payment, current Mira Core
  price, service obligation, enrollment, or accepted commercial offer. No amounts
  or commercial configurations are imported. Their real-world status is unverified.
- The named adaptive-intake fixture labels missing concrete evidence and thin
  household capacity Provisional. The readiness checklist treats required gaps
  as Hold. The local intake adopts the stricter distinction; this does not repair
  or retrospectively reclassify the upstream record.
- Some approval examples use safe-to-use language or leave evidence permission
  unresolved in a provisional case. Here, readiness, exact use approval, and
  educational suitability remain separate. Required handling gaps produce Hold.
- The naming guide repeats Learning Core for different layers and contains
  conflicting usage instructions. Here it names the local project; family plans
  and selected records are described plainly. No business/artifact identity is inferred.
- Installation and live-cycle documents propose different sequencing and levels
  of capture. The local plan keeps dependency order and adaptable stages, without
  activating calendars, capture quotas, or mandatory profile persistence.
- Ordinary evidence alignment is useful judgment, not a validated numerical rule.
  Self-enhancement assertions, acceptance labels, and usefulness scores are not
  local measured outcomes. Upstream approvals and fictional cases confer no authority.
- Commercial designs, app catalogs, Khan-specific integrations, embodied-AI designs,
  and OB1 architecture remain deferred ancestry. Referenced material outside the
  60-file directory was not comprehensively reviewed; product claims, external
  implementations, and private accounting were not verified. No numerical product
  claims, named learner context, or operational configuration was imported.
