# Learning Core

Current as of: 2026-09-13. Status: local preparation package; no learner intake,
educational intervention, commercial offer, or recurring activity activated.

Learning Core supports a coherent, increasingly learner-owned life of learning.
Plans, questions, connections, and selected work should remain intelligible and
usable across home, school, books, projects, and other settings, including when
ongoing assistance ends. A plan or portfolio is a supporting artifact, not the
whole learner or a replacement for school, relationships, or human judgment.

## Evidence and responsibilities

These methods are adapted from the pinned Anyang project, with complete
[source accounting](source-map.md). The sources describe methods, proposals, and
examples; they do not establish local learner facts or improved learning outcomes.
The current objective is an inspectable preparation package that an adult can use
to scope a later task. There is no enrolled family or effective learner profile here.

Parents or guardians retain decisions within their authority over participation,
drafting, plan use, child-facing language, resources, and private records. Learners
may choose, decline, correct, and help select what matters without acquiring adult
responsibilities. Teachers, clinicians, and institutions retain their respective
roles. This package supplies no diagnosis, grade placement, mastery finding, or
educational compliance determination. Approval does not establish suitability or
effectiveness.

[Treasury](../../docs/skill-drafts/mira-treasury/SKILL.md) may assess proposed time,
capacity, and resource requirements, keeping estimates, proposed allocations,
authorized commitments, and observed use distinct. Workshop uses
[Mira Work](../../docs/skill-drafts/mira-work/SKILL.md) for consequential authorized
preparation. Neither practice acquires guardian, teacher, clinical, or institutional
authority. A later blocked human decision can use existing
[Elicitation](../../docs/skill-drafts/elicitation/SKILL.md). No new intake command,
database, catalog, or mandatory Memory inventory is required.

## Private work and later decisions

Keep these templates blank in Git. Completed profiles, family responses,
assessments, photographs, and approval records belong only in an explicitly
approved private location outside Git. A pseudonym does not make a family record
publishable. Establish handling permission before collecting details; selective
retention and learner choice matter more than capture volume. Portability never
implies continuous observation or automatic sharing.

A later task must establish the deciding humans, purpose, relevant evidence,
private handling, and exact action permission. Ready is a readiness judgment,
not approval. Thirty days and optional review rhythms are planning structures,
not schedules. No installation, automatic journal or ledger entry, monitoring,
correspondence, or commercial commitment follows from this package.
