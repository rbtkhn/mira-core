# Adaptable 30-day planning shell

Blank parent-reviewable template. Thirty days and the stages below are adaptable
planning structures, not activated dates, schedules, or required review cadence.
Completed copies belong in explicitly approved private storage outside Git.
Use [intake](intake.md) and [evidence and approval](evidence-and-approval.md) first.

## Draft scope

- Purpose and parent direction:
- Proposed planning window, if useful:
- Draft version and date:
- Readiness state and rationale:
- Verified input/profile references and versions:
- Separate permission to draft:
- Unresolved questions and labeled assumptions:
- Current plan-use approval scope or pending disposition:
- Child-facing language approval scope or pending disposition:

Readiness is not approval. A Hold leaves the learner-specific plan undrafted.
A Provisional draft leaves unsupported personalization open. Neither a template
default nor a fictional example supplies learner facts.

## Selection and review

- Learner choice available, including decline or pause:
- Resources selected using [resource-selection.md](resource-selection.md):
- Why each recommendation follows from its attributed input:
- What would weaken the working interpretation:
- Selected evidence categories, omissions, and handling permission:
- Optional review prompt and timing, if the parent chooses:
- Parent changes and affected sections:
- Revised plan and matching approval reference:
- Next direction or pause decision:

More activity is not proof of mastery. Leave educational suitability and any
needed professional judgment open rather than claiming them from approval.
Close a later preparation task with the [continuity handoff](continuity.md),
including what is usable independently and what still requires assistance.
