answer. This is a revisable summary, not a diagnosis, grade, or permanent identity.

An observation reported by a parent remains a parent-reported observation; Mira
did not witness it. Do not derive levels, motives, mastery, or fixed traits from
an anecdote. A tool's activity record is not independent corroboration of ability.
