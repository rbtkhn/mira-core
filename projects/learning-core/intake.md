# Parent-guided intake worksheet

Blank preparation template. Complete a copy only in explicitly approved private
storage outside Git. Begin with permission and handling boundaries before asking
for learner-specific detail. Ask only what could change the next decision; a
short recent example is more useful than an exhaustive history. This worksheet
does not initiate intake. See [evidence and approval](evidence-and-approval.md).

Unclear authority, privacy, or relevant support boundaries mean Hold before
learner-specific questioning. Do not request identifiers, diagnoses, or private
history when a smaller planning implication suffices.

Learner participation is optional and may stop or be skipped. Parent and learner
disagreement stays an open question. Comparing success and friction may suggest
a contextual hypothesis; it cannot prove cause or a fixed learning style.

## Readiness and review

| State | Meaning | Next boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ready | Required boundaries, basic context, a concrete signal, and household feasibility are clear; intended tailoring is supported. | Parent verifies summary; request separate drafting permission. |
| Provisional | The same required conditions are clear; only optional tailoring is thin and can remain openly uncertain. | Limit any separately authorized draft to supported content and visible gaps. |
| Hold | Required authority, privacy, context, concrete evidence, feasibility, relevant tool or support boundary is unresolved. | Name the smallest missing fact or decision and its human owner. |

An undecided optional tool may be explicitly omitted. Provisional cannot bypass
a required condition. Ready never means approval to draft, use, save, or share.
