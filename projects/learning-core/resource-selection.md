# Resource selection

Select resources for a named, parent-reviewed purpose, using household reality
and attributed evidence. This guide endorses no app, book title, curriculum,
commercial offer, or intervention. Any future specific recommendation needs
current source review and the relevant human decisions.

## Tool-neutral criteria

Consider available materials before proposing additions. Compare fit to the
question, learner interest and choice, accessibility, required supervision,
preparation and cleanup, time, cost, privacy, screen boundaries, and ease of
stopping or continuing without assistance. Keep unknowns visible.

Availability is not suitability. Interest is not approval. A product's internal
sequencing does not decide whether that product belongs in the household plan.
Leave an undecided optional tool out without replacement pressure. Resource
estimates remain proposals until the responsible human authorizes a commitment.

## Optional reading basket

A small selection near a comfortable reading place may make choice easier. Treat
that as a practical hypothesis to review, not an established effect for a learner.
Use books already accessible when possible. Include familiar choices, interest-led
material, a shared read-aloud, and supported challenge only where wanted; no fixed
count or inferred reading level is required. The learner may reread, abandon,
change format, or decline. Do not turn every selection into a test.

When useful, notice what was chosen, what invited a question, what needed support,
and what was repeatedly avoided. Review options to keep, replace, share aloud,
or try later. Context matters: fatigue, unfamiliarity, access, and adult support
can all affect an episode. Do not convert the basket into a diagnosis or a full
reading curriculum.

## Interpreting activity

App time, streaks, scores, completion, and book counts describe activity within
their limits. Activity is not proof of mastery. A selected artifact or a report
of ease may inform a question; it cannot alone establish ability or causation.
Any change should state its rationale, contrary evidence, and parent-review scope.
No transition between products or developmental target is inherited from upstream.

Keep sensing, catalog imports, account integrations, and child-facing AI outside
this package. Their historical designs are identified in [source-map.md](source-map.md).
