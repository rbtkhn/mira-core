# Continuity without ongoing assistance

The practical test is whether an adult can recover the purpose, current decisions,
selected work, and next question without the originating conversation or service.
This guide prepares that handoff; no family handoff or subscription is activated.

## What should remain usable

Leave the current approved plan, its rationale and limits, the learner's selected
questions or work, and a clear next choice in a readable form the family controls.
Use ordinary printable text and existing physical materials when they suffice.
No paid service, proprietary memory system, or continuous observation should be
necessary to understand what the family already chose to keep.

Record dependencies honestly: an account, inaccessible file, adult supervision,
professional judgment, or unfinished approval may still be needed. Do not claim
an export is independent merely because files exist. Check that the intended
recipient can open permitted materials and understand their scope, if that check
is separately authorized. Never add private sharing to make portability easier.

## Pause and resume

Pause means no automatic follow-up, catch-up work, capture, or commercial conversion.
Keep only authorized records; preserve a short open question if useful and allowed.
Ending assistance must not erase or deliberately weaken the family's usable work.

At a later authorized resume, recover the last approved version, confirm current
authority and handling, and ask what materially changed. Mark stale or unavailable
evidence rather than reconstruct it as fact. Use [intake](intake.md) only for
decision-changing gaps; do not repeat a full intake as ceremony. Reconcile changes
through [evidence and approval](evidence-and-approval.md) before affected use.

A handoff can be complete while learning effectiveness remains unknown. The
[worked examples](worked-examples.md) demonstrate this distinction synthetically.
