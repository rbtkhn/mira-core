# Fictional adult-operator walkthroughs

All three cases below are explicitly fictional. Adults can read them as document
reviews without interviewing a learner, creating a family record, or trying an
activity. Mock evidence never becomes learner fact. The stated permissions exist
only inside each fiction and authorize no real action.

## 1. Supported draft and parent-change reconciliation

Fictional input S1: the deciding guardian authorizes preparation from a verified
summary. Discussion and temporary handling are defined. The guardian reports a
recent voluntary sketch of a leaf and a separate stalled activity after unclear
instructions; the parent describes the context and what helped. Basic setting,
available materials, feasible time, supervision, exclusions, and pause boundaries
are clear. No app is proposed; optional learner participation may be declined.

The adult reviewer classifies Ready for this narrow preparation scope. Ready is
not approval; S1's separate drafting permission permits a draft. A proposed
choice between describing and drawing something already noticed is traceable to
S1, not evidence of a fixed learning style. The draft asks whether a clearer first
step helps; it makes no outcome claim. Use remains pending parent review.

Fictional version P1 proposes optional selected photos and written reflections.
The guardian requests paper-only work, no photos, and no required reflection.
The adult revises P2 and the matching approval record A2:

| Surface | P1 proposal | P2 / A2 after fictional review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence selection | Optional photos and written reflection | Paper-only selection; no photos; reflection may be declined. |
| Activity wording | Record a reflection | Invite a choice; no required written response. |
| Handling | Proposed digital copy | No digital learner evidence; physical handling only within the fictional guardian's specified scope. |
| Approval | Use pending | Fictional guardian confirms exact P2 wording and limited use; sharing remains unapproved. |

The adult compares every affected section against A2; P1 is superseded and its
photo suggestion is inactive. A plan still containing photos fails this review
even if A2 has every field filled. Real saving or use has not occurred.

Handoff result: P2, A2, their rationale, and the open question are understandable
without a chat. Physical selection needs the family's participation; any further
change needs its deciding human. The scripted approval proves neither educational
suitability nor improved learning.

## 2. Provisional draft with an optional gap

Fictional input P1: guardian authority, drafting permission, privacy, concrete
recent evidence, basic context, household capacity, and support boundaries are
explicit. The parent reports a recent choice to revisit a familiar picture book.
The exact next book is unknown. Existing books are available; apps are undecided
and explicitly omitted. No new storage or sharing is authorized.

The adult classifies Provisional because only optional tailoring is thin. The
draft offers a flexible opportunity to choose among existing materials, leaves
the title blank, and labels future fit uncertain. It does not invent a reading
level or select an app. The proposed 30-day structure remains adaptable; no
calendar is activated. Plan use is still pending the guardian's exact review.

Handoff result: the parent can understand the proposal and open book-choice
question independently. Further tailoring may need new input, but no subscription,
database, or mandatory profile persistence is necessary. Classifying this Ready
with a fabricated title or treating Provisional as permission to use fails review.

## 3. Hold for unresolved authority and handling

Fictional input H1: an adult asks for a quick plan, but the deciding guardian is
unclear and permission to retain the answers has not been established. Basic
learner evidence and realistic supervision are also absent.

The adult records only the permitted synthetic review result: Hold. There is no
learner-specific draft, copied profile, child-facing question, or saved family
record. The next request identifies the deciding guardian and the permitted
handling before collecting more detail. Once those are resolved in a separately
authorized task, the missing basic evidence and feasibility still need review.
Provisional cannot bypass them; enthusiasm or urgency does not supply permission.

Handoff result: the unresolved conditions and deciding-human requirement are
clear. A pause is a complete preparation outcome here. No schedule, outreach,
outside-support contact, or automatic resume is created.

## Review result boundary

These walkthroughs exercise [intake](intake.md), [approval reconciliation](evidence-and-approval.md),
and [continuity](continuity.md). Check that a reviewer can reject the wrong outcome,
not merely find completed fields. Passing document checks establishes coverage
and integrity; it does not demonstrate better learning or future operator behavior.
