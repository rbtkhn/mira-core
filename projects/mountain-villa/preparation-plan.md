# Mountain Villa preparation plan

Status: dependency-ordered preparation, not an activated 30-day schedule.
The two upstream sequences are consolidated into these four stages. No site
visit, assessment, monitor, calendar, or operating review starts here.

## 1. Establish context

Use [intake.md](intake.md) to identify the owner question, permitted evidence,
private storage, and authority already supplied. Exit with a sufficiently clear
scope and explicit missing facts. Context acceptance does not authorize inspection.

## 2. Identify evidence gaps

Within authorized access, identify dates, owners, and missing records for relevant
asset, maintenance, continuity, and seasonal questions. Water, septic, utilities,
access, wildfire, and structures are possible review categories, not diagnoses
or assertions about this property's systems. Prepare professional questions;
do not perform technical assessment. Exit with a bounded private evidence map.

## 3. Prepare prioritized decisions

Use the [risk template](risk-register.md). Consequence precedes convenience;
evidence gaps remain distinct from confirmed conditions. Treasury compares
estimates and capacity in their own units. Surface consequential work that
optional retreat or sale presentation might displace. Exit with a small
owner-ready decision set, proposed limits, and exclusions.

## 4. Prepare the first owner review

Assemble the [review guide](operating-review.md), evidence references, and
[decision template](decision-log.md). Name the exact review scope and any missing
authority before conducting it. Reuse existing explicit authorization if it
covers the review; otherwise stop at the prepared packet. A review decision
does not authorize external or physical action beyond its exact scope.

Missing evidence blocks dependent conclusions only. Stage completion proves no
mitigation, preparedness, or business benefit. Weekly, monthly, and seasonal
cadences in the sources remain historical suggestions; any recurrence requires
an explicit future instruction. Return to [README.md](README.md).
