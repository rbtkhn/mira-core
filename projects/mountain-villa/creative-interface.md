# Mountain Villa creative interface

Status: guidance, not activation of a creative role, runtime, production
capacity, property access, or physical work.

## Frame the task

Start with the owner's intended experience in their own words, exact area or
presentation surface, audience, sources, artifact, resource limits, and next
decision. Use [Elicitation](../../docs/skill-drafts/elicitation/SKILL.md) only for
materially missing human choices. Explore atmosphere, palette, art, presentation,
and maintenance implications without technical diagnosis or construction specifications.

Identify relevant safety, maintenance, continuity, and seasonal obligations
and competing resource demands. Make the tradeoff visible to the owner. Approval
of optional design does not resolve an underlying concern. Hold dependent
production if necessary risk context or the owner's priority disposition is missing.

## Protect source and output

Classify each independently: ordinary, property-private, security-sensitive,
or disclosure-sensitive. Use the more restrictive classification. Polishing or
removing identifiers does not itself authorize a privacy downgrade. Name approved
storage, recipients, retention, source rights, and exclusions. Private layouts,
access, vulnerabilities, calendars, photographs, owner preferences, and sale
records must remain outside Git.

## Ownership and production

The owner selects direction. Workshop uses
[Mira Work](../../docs/skill-drafts/mira-work/SKILL.md) for consequential authorized
artifact production and appropriate existing tools. Treasury prepares resource
tradeoffs. Neither appoints an Artistic Director nor inherits Anyang runtime,
System Engineer, or Executive Assistant roles.

Concept selection is not permission to build, buy, hire, host, publish, contact
buyers, or change the property. Professionals retain architectural, engineering,
safety, insurance, valuation, legal, and disclosure conclusions. Water quality,
boundaries, condition, and improvements require appropriate current evidence
before external claims. No design establishes safety, compliance, market value,
or sale readiness.

Return the objective, artifact, sources/rights, sensitivity, storage, measured
resource use, limitations, and next owner decision. Preserve missing authority
without inventing a new production command. Use the [review guide](operating-review.md).
