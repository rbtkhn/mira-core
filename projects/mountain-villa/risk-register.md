Rank consequence first, then urgency, dependency, evidence quality, reversibility,
and human authority. Cost informs feasibility; cheap optional work must not
outrank a consequential unresolved concern merely because it is easier. Explain
tradeoffs in prose. The upstream arithmetic score is not adopted as a validated
safety model. A possible failure chain is not a diagnosis; a repair receipt alone
does not prove safety, compliance, insurance acceptance, or reduced risk.

## Hypothetical examples — not property records

- A fictional owner supplies an undated maintenance note. Establish its date and
  relevance before drawing a conclusion about present condition.
- A fictional presentation proposal competes with a continuity question. Make
  the resource conflict visible for the owner; infer neither resolved risk nor
  approved improvement.
