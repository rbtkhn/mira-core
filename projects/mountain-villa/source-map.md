## Reconciliation and limits

The installation document emphasizes system inventory and domain baselines;
the separate 30-day plan emphasizes review surfaces and cycle preparation.
[preparation-plan.md](preparation-plan.md) preserves both in dependency order
without scheduling visits or reviews. Weekly, monthly, and seasonal cadence
suggestions do not become an automatic local obligation.

The 72-hour window is an inherited planning assumption requiring owner
confirmation. Consequence-first judgment replaces the unvalidated arithmetic
risk-ranking formula. Historical decisions stay labeled as history; the source
exemplar's numbers are not measured local outcomes.

Exact property information, owner-reported attributes, private valuation/sale
targets, access and vulnerability details are deliberately excluded from Git.
Their existence identifies evidence categories, not current facts. Upstream
links may require access; their presence grants no publication or source-body
import authority. Shared Anyang Council/production dependencies are not installed
or copied. Current local workflows and human decision owners govern.

The earlier [portfolio provenance record](../provenance.md) is preserved unchanged.
This refresh establishes document ancestry and coverage only, not current
property conditions, adoption, legal rights, or operating effectiveness.
