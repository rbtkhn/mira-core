# Mira Projects

Created: 2026-09-07
Status: local development home; first pilot prepared, not executed

Mira Core is the primary home for new development of these inherited projects.
Their purposes give research a place to encounter practical constraints and
observable results. The first question is whether an idea helps people complete
acceptable work with less total effort, fewer errors, and no hidden supervision.

Start with the [operational lab](lab/README.md), its [hypotheses](lab/hypotheses.md),
and the [pilot queue](lab/pilot-queue.md). Sources from Nate Herk, Nate B. Jones,
Moonshots, and Innermost Loop are eligible; no source receives a quota or a
presumption of correctness.

## Inheritance and ownership

[Provenance](provenance.md) identifies the inspected Anyang revision. Anyang is
read-only ancestry, not a second automatically synchronized development home.
New project hypotheses, designs, and results are maintained here. That choice
does not transfer a business, client relationship, account, or operating role.

These are newly written orientation records, not imported project bodies. Old
approval records and plans do not become current instructions. The existing
[Grace Gems admission matrix](grace-mar/grace-gems/admission-matrix.md) remains controlling
for that project's upstream material. Private evidence stays outside Git.

The projects retain different purposes. Human participation may be valuable
learning or authorship rather than waste. State what work is being reduced and
what judgment must remain before interpreting an efficiency result.
