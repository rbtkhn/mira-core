## Shared practices and project boundaries

Workshop uses [Mira Work](../docs/skill-drafts/mira-work/SKILL.md) for consequential
authorized execution. [Treasury](../docs/skill-drafts/mira-treasury/SKILL.md)
prepares resource judgments. Each project retains its evidence and deciding humans.
Transfer the method with its provenance and limits. Re-establish fit and
permission in the receiving project. Facts, approvals, commitments, and private
records do not travel automatically. Each project's stricter domain controls apply.

### Cross-project method transfer

Use this procedure when a task actually adapts a method or result from another
project. Merely naming projects or using a shared skill does not trigger a review.
This is guidance for judgment and handoffs, not technical access isolation.

1. Identify the originating method, source reference and version, original problem,
   and demonstrated limits. Separate a proposal, fictional test, observed result,
   and adopted practice; do not promote one into another.
2. Name the receiving problem and explain what fits, what changes, and what does
   not transfer. A result in one context is not proof of effectiveness elsewhere.
3. Identify permitted inputs and the deciding human for the next action. Separate
   source access from permission to reuse, copy, save, or share. Sanitization alone
   does not authorize reuse. Estimates, balances, and commitments remain attributed
   to their owning project; shared Treasury support does not pool authority.
4. Continue within the receiving task's established permission. Reuse an adequate
   task or handoff record and reference its evidence instead of copying it. Do not
   ask again for permission already supplied for that exact action. Recheck only
   materially changed conditions, evidence, scope, or authority.

A short explanation in the existing task or handoff is enough:
**source method and limits; receiving purpose and adaptation; permitted inputs;
deciding human and authorized next action**. No separate form, registry, mandatory
receipt, or automatic record is required. Persistence follows the owning workflow.

If a source is unavailable, mark its claims unverified and avoid attributing a
reconstructed method as inspected. If fit or permission is missing, continue
independent generic preparation already within scope; pause only the dependent
private-data use, unsupported claim, or consequential action. Keep changed or
withdrawn permission visible. Do not reuse a previous approval for a new purpose.

A completed explanation cannot prove sound judgment or successful transfer.
