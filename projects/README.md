authorized execution. [Treasury](../docs/skill-drafts/mira-treasury/SKILL.md)

fit and permission must be established. Facts, approvals, commitments, and private
records do not transfer automatically. Results in one context do not establish
effectiveness elsewhere. Private evidence remains outside Git with its owner.
