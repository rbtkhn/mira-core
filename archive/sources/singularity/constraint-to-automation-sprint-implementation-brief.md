## Purpose

Turn one painful business bottleneck into a measured AI workflow improvement.

## Core Promise

We find one workflow costing you time or revenue, automate the smallest useful version, and prove whether the number moved.

## Best Buyers

- Founder-led service business
- Agency
- Local operator
- Small ops team with obvious manual leakage

## Ideal First Use Cases

- Lead intake and follow-up
- Proposal or quote generation
- Customer support triage
- Weekly reporting
- CRM cleanup
- Content repurposing
- Internal knowledge retrieval

## Delivery Shape

1. Diagnose one constraint with clear volume, pain, owner, and measurable before-state.
2. Define the metric: time per task, response time, conversion rate, missed leads, error rate, or weekly labor hours.
3. Build the minimum AI workflow with Claude, Codex, or browser automation as needed, with human review where risk demands it.
4. Run a before/after test against the same workflow after deployment.
5. Package the win into a reusable playbook, template, checklist, or retainer candidate.

## Offer Ladder

| stage | shape |
|---|---|
| Audit | Fixed-fee workflow constraint scan. |
| Sprint | One measured automation shipped in 1-2 weeks. |
| Retainer | Maintain, improve, and expand the workflow stack. |
| Asset | Reusable templates or workflows for repeated deployment. |

## Measurement Plan

- Baseline manual time per run
- Weekly workflow volume
- Error or rework count
- Revenue or lead leakage where visible
- Post-automation time per run
- Human review burden
- Adoption rate after handoff

## Go/No-Go Filter

- Strong yes: painful, repeated, measurable, owned by one team, and safe to automate partially.
- Maybe: strategic but hard to measure.
- No: one-off task, unclear owner, no baseline, or high-risk without review controls.

## Innermost Loop Angle

This is a tight operating loop: detect constraint, intervene, measure, retain the workflow pattern, and feed the result back into the Applied AI Opportunity Ledger.
