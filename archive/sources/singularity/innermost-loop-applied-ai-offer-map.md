## Operating Interpretation

The strongest product is the **Constraint-to-Automation Sprint**. It should be the anchor because it has the cleanest buyer pain and the clearest measurable outcome. The other five offers support it:

- The **Automation Pricing Clinic** teaches how to quote and defend the work.
- The **Five Demand-Ready Automations Catalog** supplies repeatable entry points.
- The **Workflow Asset Productization** offer prevents one-off work from staying one-off.
- The **Cheap Agent Swarm Build Lab** supplies a lower-cost execution pattern.
- The **Agent Tool Selection Matrix** keeps the operator from wasting cycles on the wrong tool.

Together these form an Innermost Loop commercialization stack: detect applied AI opportunity, choose a buyer pain, package the intervention, build with the right agent loop, measure the result, and convert the result into reusable workflow assets.
