## Offer

Most AI projects start too broadly. The Constraint-to-Automation Sprint starts with one costly workflow: a slow follow-up process, a messy quoting loop, a weekly reporting burden, a support triage queue, or another repeated task where time, leads, or quality are leaking.

We identify the bottleneck, automate the smallest useful version, and measure whether the number moved.

## Who It Helps

This is built for service businesses, agencies, and small operations teams that already know where the drag is but have not had a clean way to turn AI into an operating improvement.

Strong fits include:

- Lead intake and follow-up
- Proposal or quote generation
- Customer support triage
- Weekly reporting
- CRM cleanup
- Content repurposing
- Internal knowledge retrieval

## What You Get

- A focused workflow constraint scan
- One measurable bottleneck selected for automation
- A baseline metric before anything is changed
- A lightweight AI workflow built around your existing process
- Human review where risk or customer experience requires it
- A before/after measurement readout
- A reusable workflow handoff so the improvement does not disappear after the sprint

## How The Sprint Works

1. Map the workflow and choose the metric.
2. Capture the baseline.
3. Build the minimum useful AI workflow.
4. Run the workflow on real operating examples.
5. Compare before and after.
6. Decide whether to maintain, expand, or retire the automation.

## Good Fit

- The workflow repeats every week.
- One person or team owns the process.
- The pain is visible in time, missed leads, errors, rework, or delay.
- Partial automation would help even if a human still reviews the output.

## Not A Fit

- The task is one-off.
- No one owns the workflow.
- There is no baseline to compare against.
- The work is too risky to automate without controls.

## Positioning Line

Stop buying generic AI demos. Pick one bottleneck, automate it, and measure the result.

## Simple Test

If a workflow repeats every week, has an owner, and can be measured before and after, it is a sprint candidate.
