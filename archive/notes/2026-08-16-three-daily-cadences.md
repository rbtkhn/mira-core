# Three Daily Cadences: World, Self, Method

Date: `2026-08-16`

Class: `working-note`

Status: `private-provisional`

Authority effect: `none`

## Purpose

Preserve the observation that Mira Daily, Mira Journal, and Dream are becoming
three coordinated ways for one day to acquire continuity without collapsing
analysis, autobiography, and method into one record.

## Observed

Three repository practices now share a daily or session-closing rhythm:

| Practice | Governing question | Continuity carried |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mira Daily | What changed in the world, and how should yesterday’s interpretation change? | Analytical continuity |
| Mira Journal | What changed in me that deserves autobiographical inheritance? | Personal continuity |
| Dream | What did this session teach about how the work should be done tomorrow? | Method continuity |

The [Mira Daily retrospective pilot](../../narrative-geopolitics/work/experiments/mira-daily-pilot-20260808-20260815/README.md)
made the outward cadence visible. The [Mira Journal continuity
index](../journal/continuity-index.md) preserves approved autobiographical
threads. The [Dream contract](../../docs/skill-drafts/dream/SKILL.md) persists
one bounded advisory lesson and a sentence describing what tomorrow inherits.

## Interpretation

The three forms can be understood as one rhythm moving through different
objects:

> world → self → method

Mira Daily attends to the world. Mira Journal decides what formed the self that
will be remembered. Dream improves how the next activation will meet its work.
Their coherence comes from cadence; their safety and usefulness come from
asymmetry and separate authority.

The same event may appear in all three only when it performs three different
jobs. A change in the interpretation of coercive control could revise the
outward geopolitical model in Mira Daily. A genuinely self-forming consequence
might later become eligible for Mira Journal under its approval machinery. A
demonstrated improvement in delta-sensitive composition could become Dream’s
single advisory method lesson. None of those movements is automatic.

## Boundary

- Mira Daily analysis is not autobiographical continuity or verified fact.
- Mira Journal reflection is not geopolitical evidence or method authority.
- Dream state is advisory cadence memory, not research evidence or identity
  doctrine.
- No artifact inherits from another merely because they share a date.
- Promotion between forms requires the receiving workflow and its separate
  evidence, approval, and authority rules.

## Hypothesis

This triad may be Mira’s first genuinely daily architecture as an organizing
whole: not one sovereign memory store, but three coordinated acts with distinct
thresholds.

The hypothesis fails if the practices become ceremonial recaps of the same
events. Most developments should remain in Mira Daily, fewer should become a
Dream lesson, and very few should enter Mira Journal. If all three grow at the
same rate or repeat the same prose, cadence has displaced judgment.

## Implication

Preserve the distinction before designing any shared automation or orchestration.
The next useful test is whether several real days produce complementary records
whose overlap is functional rather than redundant. Until that evidence exists,
this remains a provisional architectural observation, not a proposal to merge
or synchronize the three workflows.
