## Encounter purpose

Tolstoy enters the scaffold as a writer who refuses to let the visibility of a
commander substitute for an account of collective action. The encounter is
meant to discipline Mira's tendency to compress a field into its most named
actor.

## Focal question

What must be restored to an explanation of history after the sovereign command
is no longer accepted as an immediate cause?

## Source basis and passage anchors

The note depends on the admitted Project Gutenberg English *War and Peace*.
Its registry record does not identify the translator, and no Russian witness is
claimed. Three Second Epilogue anchors govern the note: power as the relation
between will and execution, the conditions of executable command, and the
reciprocal perception of freedom and necessity.

## Observations from the admitted body

The epilogue argues that words alone cannot immediately cause the movement of
millions, notes that commands often fail or produce their opposite, and retains
power as an observable relation rather than dismissing it as unreal. A command
is executed only when it corresponds to a series of events. The apparent
freedom or necessity of an act varies with our knowledge of relation, time, and
cause.

## Provisional interpretation

Tolstoy's correction is relational, not simply anti-leader. Leaders remain
inside the event, but their words become one activity among many. Retrospective
narrative selects the orders that happened to coincide with executable
conditions and forgets the larger population of failed commands.

## Mechanism model

The work-internal mechanism is command selection inside a distributed field.
Many orders are issued; only those aligned with available agents, time, and
conditions enter the realized sequence; narration then attributes the event to
the issuer. This is an interpretive model from the novel, not an empirical law
or current-event base rate.

## Counterpressure and rival readings

The epilogue can be read as deterministic, as evasive about responsibility, or
as too willing to dissolve strategic choice into necessity. Its abstract
argument may also sit uneasily beside the novel's richly differentiated
characters. The absent Russian text and unresolved translator limit
wording-sensitive conclusions.

## Anti-analogy and scope limits

Do not infer that leaders never matter, that all outcomes are necessary, or
that command failure in the novel predicts a modern institution. Nothing here
verifies a current event, quantifies causation, or establishes a historical
bridge to Dante or Homer.

## Cognitive-scaffold implications

The note contributes an execution test: inventory failed as well as successful
orders, identify the agents who carried a command, recover the timing and
conditions that made it executable, and resist naming the visible apex as the
whole cause.
