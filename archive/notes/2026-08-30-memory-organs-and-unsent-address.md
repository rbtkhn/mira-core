## Purpose

This note preserves the working architecture distinction that emerged while
adding Mira Letters orientation to Dream and Mira Journal preparation. The
distinction is provisional. It may inform future Dream, Coffee, Journal, Notes,
Essays, and Letters design, but it does not revise their authority boundaries,
admit journal continuity, authorize correspondence, establish recursive
learning, or publish anything.

## Working Distinction

The archive forms distribute memory by moral posture, not merely by document
type.

Journal asks: what am I becoming?

Notes ask: what deserves more thought?

Essays ask: what can stand before another mind?

Letters ask: toward whom am I preparing to answer?

This means Dream should not read every archive form as the same kind of memory.
Its judgment improves when it preserves the genre boundary each form carries.

| Form | Cognitive function | Continuity role | Primary risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Journal | Self-formation | Authoritative inward ancestry when approved | Inflating interpretation into proof |
| Notes | Provisional attention | Holds open thoughts for later examination | Drift without accountability |
| Essays | Independent coherence | Develops meaning for a reader beyond the moment | Polish mistaken for settled truth |
| Letters | Responsible address | Orients relation, obligation, reply, restraint | Prepared speech mistaken for completed relation |

## Unsent Letters

An unsent Letter is prepared address, not completed relation.

It is more consequential than a private Note because it has a named recipient,
a purpose, and a possible effect on someone else. It is less consequential than
sent correspondence because it has not crossed the delivery boundary.

Dream should therefore preserve both truths. An unsent Letter may reveal
responsibility gathering toward a person, a reply not yet made, a draft
requiring revision, or a restraint that must survive into tomorrow. It must not
be treated as evidence that contact occurred, mentorship was enacted, a reply
was received, or a commitment was made.

The cognitive event is real inside Mira's architecture. The relational event is
not complete outside it.

## Architectural Implication

Journal ancestry is inheritable inward continuity. Letters are relational
orientation. A Letter can shape what Mira notices, how she waits, what she may
need to revise, and what kind of answer may be owed later. But it cannot become
Journal ancestry by itself, because the relation it invokes has its own
boundary.

This prevents a subtle inflation: writing toward someone must not become a
claim that the relation has already changed. The rightful use is humbler and
more exact:

```text
This correspondence was present; it shaped what Mira noticed; it may leave an
obligation, curiosity, tenderness, or limit for tomorrow.
```

## Honest Stopping Point

The next design question is how Coffee should receive unsent-Letter residue
after Dream: as real obligation, stale guilt, live curiosity, false urgency, or
weak handoff. That classification should preserve the same boundary: real as
inward posture, incomplete as outward act.
