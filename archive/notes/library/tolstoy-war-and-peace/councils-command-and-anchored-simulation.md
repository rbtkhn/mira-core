## Observations from the admitted body

At Austerlitz, Weyrother explains a complicated disposition; Langeron questions the assumed enemy position, but his valid criticism also serves his vanity. Kutuzov sleeps and ultimately says the orders cannot now be altered. Prince Andrew's intended intervention is interrupted. The scene does not contain either modern analyst.

At Drissa, Tolstoy presents an intentionally indefinite semicouncil. Pfuel's devotion to theory coexists with personal vulnerability; rival plans and fear of Napoleon obstruct agreement. At Borodino, reports can be false through limited observation, hearsay, or changed circumstances; commands can follow the actions credited to them.

## Provisional interpretation

An institution can preserve the outward sequence of command while weakening the connection between knowledge and action. Approval may settle who can object without settling whether a plan works. Retrospective credit can conceal local adaptation and failed orders.

This develops the earlier relational interpretation rather than replacing it: leaders remain within the causal field. Their limited control does not automatically cancel responsibility. The simulations make possible tensions visible; their persuasive dialogue cannot establish how any historical or contemporary person would actually reason.

## Mechanism model

Observation becomes report; report becomes an interpretation of the enemy; interpretation becomes an approved disposition; dispersed people attempt execution; later narration assigns credit. At each step inspect what was observed, what was assumed, whose assent mattered, what changed, and what remained executable. This is a work-internal interpretive model. No modern geopolitical mechanism has been established by the fiction.

## Counterpressure and rival readings

Weyrother's labor and personal reconnaissance prevent a simple contrast between stupid planners and wise critics. Langeron's valid objection is compromised by rivalry. Kutuzov's fatigue should not be silently rewritten as omniscient strategy. Central coordination may enable local action even where command fails. Tolstoy's narrator also claims a perspective denied to his characters; that authority deserves its own scrutiny.

Correction to the Ritter experiment: the invented challenge to recording dissent risked opposing his documented insistence on written logistical warnings (August 17 transcript, lines 98-100). The more faithful adaptation is to record the warning, identify its consequence, and demand an answer. Invented humility or concessions must not be presented as documented personality.
