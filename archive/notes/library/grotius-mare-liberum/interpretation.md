## Encounter purpose

This successor preserves the completed route-review interpretation while
placing it in the reusable cognitive-note form. It does not alter the reviewed
predecessor or its approval binding.

## Focal question

How can a legal principle be both genuinely generalizable and strategically
advantageous to the claimant who articulates it?

## Source basis and passage anchors

The note retains the admitted Latin Wikisource body and three anchors: common
use against exclusive prescription, Dutch and universal utility in Chapter
XII, and armed vindication of commercial right in Chapter XIII. Edition lineage
and an admitted translation remain unresolved.

## Observations from the admitted body

The common-use passage denies exclusive prescription over navigation. Chapter
XII explicitly joins Dutch advantage to the asserted advantage of humanity.
Chapter XIII moves from common commercial right to armed enforcement when
adjudication is unavailable.

## Provisional interpretation

The tract's universal grammar cannot be dismissed merely because it is
interested, but neither can interest be erased by abstraction. Its sharpest
tension is the conjunction of open access and a claimed right to coercive
enforcement.

## Mechanism model

The work-internal mechanism converts a positional access claim into a universal
rule capable of delegitimizing an incumbent barrier. The same argument then
supplies a legal vocabulary for force. The tract alone does not establish the
later distribution or institutional outcome of that rule.

## Counterpressure and rival readings

The Library still lacks an admitted closed-seas rival and a counterpart able to
test who gained access and who bore coercion. Sponsor context is interpretive,
not independent corroboration.

## Cross-work constellation

No new comparative work relation is asserted by this normalization successor.
Any future connection must be explicitly authored and separately grounded.

## Anti-analogy and scope limits

Do not treat the tract as current legal authority, equate open access with
noncoercion, or infer realized institutional outcomes from advocacy alone.

## Cognitive-scaffold implications

The note contributes an interested-universal test: ask whether a general rule
is valid beyond its sponsor, which barrier it removes, which capacities it
redistributes, and what coercive enforcement accompanies the opening.

## Open questions

The missing rival, translation, edition identity, and distributional test
remain the decisive counterpressure.
