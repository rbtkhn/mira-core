## Encounter purpose

This successor preserves the source-grounded encounter while placing it in the
reusable template. It keeps aesthetic perception answerable to rank, access,
and unequal freedom rather than extracting courtly style for imitation.

## Focal question

How does indirectness function when the participants in an intimate scene do
not possess equal rank, access, mobility, or control over narration?

## Source basis and passage anchors

The note retains four partial Waley translation bodies and three anchors:
Kiritsubo's favor and vulnerability, the Broom-Tree classification of women,
and screened speech as constrained control of an encounter. The Japanese text
and final parts remain absent.

## Observations from the admitted body

Favor raises Kiritsubo above her rank while exposing her to organized
resentment and decline. The Broom-Tree conversation ranks women as objects of
male report and then destabilizes the classification. A woman later uses a
screen, distance, and exact speech to control terms available within an unequal
encounter.

## Provisional interpretation

The work resists becoming a style source. Its sharper contribution is a
discipline of perceiving rank, mediated access, silence, and unequal freedom.
Aesthetic attention can expose asymmetry, conceal it, or perform both movements
at once.

## Mechanism model

The work-internal mechanism is indirect power under constraint: rank structures
who may approach, speak, withdraw, and narrate; constrained actors use form,
distance, and timing to recover partial agency without dissolving the
asymmetry.

## Counterpressure and rival readings

The missing Japanese text and final parts block a whole-work conclusion.
Effects attributed to Murasaki may belong partly to Waley's mediation, and
beautiful representation can be mistaken for ethical endorsement.

## Cross-work constellation

No new comparative work relation is asserted by this normalization successor.
Any later connection must preserve the distinction between attention,
imitation, and romanticized coercion.

## Anti-analogy and scope limits

Do not route character speech as author doctrine or courtly asymmetry as a
relationship model. Partial translated coverage cannot sustain a total claim
about the work.

## Cognitive-scaffold implications

The note contributes an access test: ask who can enter, speak, withdraw,
classify, and narrate, then examine how indirect form changes agency without
pretending to equalize the field.

## Open questions

The absent final parts, Japanese witnesses, and Waley-specific effects remain
the primary rereading requirements.
