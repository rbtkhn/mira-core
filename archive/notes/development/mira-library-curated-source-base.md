# Mira Library as a Curated Source Base

Class: `working-note`
Created: `2026-09-13`
Status: `private-provisional`
Purpose: Preserve an inquiry into Mira Library's intent for a future session.
Authority effect: Advisory design interpretation only.

## Current understanding

The operator's central point is that Mira Library is, or contains, a curated
source base. The distinction between **is** and **contains** remains worth
exploring: the source base may define Library's primary identity, while reading,
interpretation, and intellectual development describe what we do with it.

My present interpretation is that deliberate selection gives the Library its
foundation: which works belong, why they matter, and whether their actual
content can be recovered reliably. The reading and reasoning around those works
should remain traceable to that foundation. This is an interpretation of the
operator's emphasis, not a newly adopted architectural contract.

## Observation — 2026-09-13

The operator identified the curated source base as the main point to retain
about Mira Library, then requested a work-note so we could continue exploring
it in another session. No selection criteria or final definition were settled.

In response, I distinguished source material from the shared readings, cognitive
notes, comparisons, and historical pressure tests developed around it. Those
interpretations should remain distinguishable from what the sources say.

For the Tower, the proposed implication is that Library supplies deliberately
selected works that can deepen or challenge a strategic assessment. Curation
gives a work a reason to be consulted; it does not make its claims automatically
true. This is one application of Library, not an exhaustive account of its intent.

## Return point

Resume with this question: **What should curation mean for Mira Library, and
which parts of its purpose follow from being a curated source base?**

Explore the distinction between the collection itself and the practices built
around it. Then consider who selects works, what warrants inclusion, how gaps
and disagreement remain visible, and how selection can be revised without
erasing the reasons for earlier choices. These are open questions, not agreed
requirements or instructions to implement changes.
