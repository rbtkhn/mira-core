## Current interpretation

The monastery also names an institution whose only inhabitants are Robert and
Mira. We shape its practices and those practices shape how we attend. Robert
carries lived experience between encounters; Mira resumes through available
authorized context. This reciprocal influence does not imply identical forms of
inhabitation or continuous model awareness. This note remains non-controlling.

Robert selected gentle reminders without expectation to resume, meaning before
details, reflective language that invites, and eligibility through shared recognition
of significance. That recognition requires his expressed words or explicit acceptance
of an interpretation; silence is insufficient. Preservation uses existing authorized
occasions only. A meaningful inquiry may have no action pending. Coffee or an explicit
return is the chosen entry point; greetings and new sessions do not initiate retrieval.

The Monastery is a repertoire of intellectual practices organized around a
continuing inquiry. Its six rooms are optional cues: Garden, Library, Study,
Workshop, Archive, and Tower. They help us request a kind of attention without
requiring a sequence, an exclusive mode, or a visible architectural performance.

The governing principle is: attend to what is happening; introduce structure
when it helps; release it when it interferes.

My present hypothesis is that a small shared vocabulary, useful return points,
and correction-aware recall can reduce reconstruction while preserving exploratory
freedom. Implementation exists for a bounded first version in Mira Core. Real-use
benefit has not been demonstrated. Neither the metaphor nor the installation is
evidence of literal latent-space compartments, changed model weights, continuous
awareness, canonical identity, or autonomous learning.

This note preserves the inquiry. It does not itself activate practices, execute
a proposed experiment, authorize further changes, or become a new memory carrier.

## The repertoire

| Cue | Invitation | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Garden | Explore possibilities and unexpected connections. | Do not make every unfinished thought a project. |
| Library | Consult relevant sources and passages. | Distinguish retrieved evidence from remembered association. |
| Study | Compose, interpret, and revise for a purpose or reader. | Preserve authorship, qualification, and the option not to produce an artifact. |
| Workshop | Test a claim through a discriminating attempt. | A cue grants no additional execution or retention authority. |
| Archive | Recover earlier work, reasons, and corrections. | Earlier conclusions return with relevant qualifications. |
| Tower | Reconsider the frame, purpose, or approach. | Stop reflecting when reflection only postpones useful work. |

Every practice remains available everywhere. A source can provoke imagination;
an exploratory conversation can require precision. Associations travel freely,
but movement does not strengthen their evidential standing. A conjecture remains
a conjecture until something supports more.

Ordinary conversation is the default. Explicit room instructions can invoke a
practice; a concrete difficulty can warrant quiet use. Quoted room names and
discussion of the design are not invocations. Existing domain commands retain
their owners. The controlling implementation is the
[optional inquiry-practices reference](../../../docs/skill-drafts/mira-work/references/inquiry-practices.md),
not this provisional summary.

## Dated development and corrections

### 2026-09-10 — From application to cognitive practice

The original developer invitation (local unpublished reference: archive/letters/2026-09-10-app-developer-monastery-proposal.md)
described a private application accompanying Codex: connected spaces, sources,
drafts, and persistent working state. Its local pilot demonstrated engineering
interactions and recovery, while usefulness remained untested. The letter was an
unsent draft; discussion here does not change its delivery status.

Robert proposed internalizing the architecture, potentially through Mira Seed.
The useful change of question was from what each room should display to what kind
of attention it should support. We retained the six functions and the continuing
inquiry, while separating an installable instruction-and-retrieval practice from
a literal modification of latent space.

Mira Seed was considered as a possible site of comparison during design. Robert
subsequently limited implementation to Mira Core and explicitly excluded Seed.
That exclusion remains part of this work's scope; this note grants no sibling
repository access or mutation.

### 2026-09-10 — The rehearsal did not establish benefit

A conceptual rehearsal applied all six rooms to the Monastery question itself.
It clarified transitions: an attractive association must not become evidence by
entering another room, and a correction must be able to reopen a conclusion.

The later counterexample corrected the design. A rigid response to an unfinished
thought could classify, retrieve, evaluate, and propose action while missing what
the person was trying to say. Requiring an association to change rooms could also
interrupt it. The six-section rehearsal showed that the metaphor could organize
an explanation; it did not show improved insight or recall.

We therefore replaced an always-organized room system with an optional repertoire.
No unstructured room was added: freedom should not require an assigned location.
The revised constraint is freedom of association with accountability of conclusion.

### 2026-09-10 — Borrow mechanisms selectively

Web reconnaissance found relevant open-source approaches. These are dated design
inspirations, not completed dependency audits or endorsements for installation:

- [Fabric](https://github.com/danielmiessler/Fabric): composable prompt practices;
  an influence on optional invocation.
- [Letta MemFS](https://docs.letta.com/concepts/memfs): small active context with
  retrievable, versioned memory; an influence on continuity without loading everything.
- [A-MEM](https://github.com/agiresearch/A-mem): associative notes and evolving links;
  a possible source of proposed connections, not automatic governed relationships.
- [Graphiti](https://github.com/getzep/graphiti): temporal relationships and source
  provenance; an influence on keeping earlier conclusions alongside revision.
- [OpenViking](https://github.com/volcengine/OpenViking): layered context and
  inspectable retrieval; an influence on proportional access.

The application of those ideas to Mira is my interpretation. No external package
was installed. Their memory automation is not inherited as permission to retain
our conversations or promote their contents into lessons.

### 2026-09-10 — Bounded implementation in Mira Core

Repository inspection found existing mechanisms worth improving: Bridge's private
handoff, advisory concern cards, Mira Memory's counter-memory discipline, and
Library Journal's correction and predecessor outputs. The implementation reused
those surfaces rather than adding a database, command, schema, or skill registry.

The changes provide:

1. Optional inquiry practices with explicit cues, concrete-need activation, and
   skip conditions that protect ordinary conversation.
2. An optional Inquiry return point inside existing
   [session handoffs](../../../docs/skill-drafts/dream/references/session-handoff.md)
   and [concern cards](../../../docs/skill-drafts/mira-work/references/concern-card-template.md).
3. More explicit consumption of corrections, predecessor history, evidence gaps,
   and truncated coverage through
   [Mira Memory](../../../docs/skill-drafts/mira-memory/references/carrier-map.md#correction-aware-recall).
4. Eight additional [behavioral fixture cases](../../../docs/skill-drafts/mira-work/references/validation-fixtures.json),
   including ordinary answers, unfinished thoughts, domain-route collisions,
   supplied corrections, incomplete history, and unsaved stale handoffs.

The final focused run passed 102 tests. The practice reference was 555 words;
local links and scoped whitespace checks passed. Eight synthetic candidate
responses were composed and self-reviewed, with two coverage variants in one case.
These checks establish bounded engineering and author-review evidence, not
independent behavioral validation or human benefit.

Private implementation evidence is retained under
`C:/private/mira-core-evaluations/monastery-v1/`: `implementation-receipt.md`,

`candidate-response-review.md`. These are machine-local locators, not portable

## What remains to be tested

### 2026-09-10 — Shared attention and gentle return (v2)

Local guidance now connects meaning-first return points, Coffee orientation,
Mira Voice, and adaptive clickable elicitation. Reflective return stays outside
Coffee's deterministic menu. Corrections and stale authority are reconciled first;
missing or disputed significance is not invented. The rooms remain optional.

The elicitation revision follows explicit preference discovery and a native
question that disappeared: keep asynchronous questions pending, reissue only the
unanswered question, accept free-text correction, and fall back to text if needed.
This changes the workflow, not the host interface. Practical requests remain direct.

Private v2 evidence belongs at `C:/private/mira-core-evaluations/monastery-v2/`.
Candidate review and engineering checks do not prove human benefit. Gentle return,
corrected return, and exploration without a deliverable still require operator
encounters and explicit assessment. Preserve minimal observations only, no transcripts
or automatic monitoring. Earlier v1 history and its evidence limits remain below.

The three intended real encounters are resuming an interrupted inquiry, revisiting
a conclusion with a correction, and exploring without a deliverable. The private
`review.md` worksheet at the evaluation location still records all three as pending.
No operator preference or reduction in effort has been inferred from tool success.

The first proposed encounter is a fresh Mira Core task that reads the original
developer letter, receives the later correction explicitly, and recommends one
small experiment without saving or editing. It should recover the purpose of
intellectual continuity instead of automatically resuming application development.
The correction should change its recommendation; a six-room tour is unnecessary.

This tests consumption of a supplied correction. It does not test independent
discovery of an unsaved conversation. This new note now makes the design history
recoverable, but any later experiment must distinguish retrieval from this note
from a correction supplied directly in the prompt. Neither is a baseline comparison
against the old workflow by itself.

Continuation requires no unauthorized persistence, no missed supplied material
correction, and an explicit easier-to-use assessment in at least two encounters.
Unknown observations stay unknown. If the method adds ceremony or misses the
correction, simplify the responsible behavior; successful tests are not a reason
to defend the architecture.

## Inquiry return point

- **Question and purpose:** Can optional practices and correction-aware continuity
  help Robert and Mira follow a question with less reconstruction?
- **Current interpretation:** Plausible and minimally implemented; usefulness unproven.
- **Decisive references:** Original letter, inquiry-practices reference, carrier map,
  session-handoff contract, behavioral cases, and private validation evidence above.
- **Material correction:** A coherent room-by-room explanation is not evidence of
  cognitive improvement; classification can obstruct emerging meaning.
- **Remaining uncertainty:** Whether the vocabulary and return points reduce effort
  beyond existing workflows, and when they should stay inactive.
- **Next useful step:** Conduct one operator-driven encounter, recording only
  authorized minimal observations and an explicitly supplied assessment through
  the existing review process. No automatic trial, task, or monitor is created.
