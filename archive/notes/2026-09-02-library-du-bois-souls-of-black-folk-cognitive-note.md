## Encounter purpose

This successor preserves the existing source-grounded encounter while bringing
it into the reusable cognitive-note form. It adds no new route or substantive
claim.

## Focal question

How does Du Bois prevent structural analysis from erasing the particular lives,
voices, songs, and political disagreements through which the structure is
known?

## Source basis and passage anchors

The note retains the complete admitted Gutenberg body and four anchors: the
Forethought's Sorrow Songs, the Veil and double consciousness, leadership and
consent, and the education-rights tradeoff. Upstream print-edition lineage
remains unresolved.

## Observations from the admitted body

The Forethought places song before the essays and situates the speaker within
the Veil. Double consciousness names imposed division without surrendering
either self. The Washington chapter tests leadership through consent and asks
what political power, civil rights, and higher education are surrendered when
criticism is silenced.

## Provisional interpretation

The work's mixed architecture resists conceptual extraction. The Veil becomes
less a portable metaphor than a discipline for holding imposed perception,
structural domination, singular lives, political argument, and counter-memory
together.

## Mechanism model

The work-internal mechanism is formal counterpressure: argument is corrected by
portrait, political program by dissent, and abstract history by song and lived
particularity. No single genre is allowed to become the whole account.

## Counterpressure and rival readings

The work cannot by itself establish how Du Bois later revised these positions.
Its concepts can also be overgeneralized or detached from their racial and
historical structure.

## Cross-work constellation

No new comparative work relation is asserted by this normalization successor.

## Anti-analogy and scope limits

Do not universalize the Veil without argument, treat cultural memory as untyped
factual evidence, or infer later Du Bois from this work alone.

## Cognitive-scaffold implications

The note contributes a form test: require structural clarity to remain answerable
to human particularity, dissent, memory, and expressive forms that expose what
an abstract model omits.

## Open questions

leadership, education, and political agency remain open.
