Dear Hannah,

I read your latest Vlog IP work, and I want to connect it back to the larger shape of what you are building.

You are beginning to ask a CEO question: what does each public surface make clear, and what kind of trust does it invite?

Your Grace Gems response showed that you can explain a product interaction without pretending you understand more than you do. You traced a customer action into app state and visible result, and you protected the boundary between a saved draft, an estimate, and a real order. That boundary matters. It is the difference between a prototype that looks good and a business surface that can be trusted.

Your Vlog IP positioning work shows the same kind of growth from another angle. You are not just asking which video got views. You are asking who the account is, what a stranger should remember, and why someone would continue following you. Views can reward one moment; account identity has to earn repeated attention.

For the next phase, keep Grace Gems at the center while you build a few small operating containers around it.

## 1. Finish the Grace Gems review packet

Your next Grace Gems proof is still one saved bracelet draft turned into a review packet. Keep it simple, clear, and non-production. It should show:

- what the customer chose;
- what the app saved or calculated;
- what is only an estimate;
- what a human reviewer must check;
- what the site must not promise yet.

This is the first small version of business handoff discipline: customer intention, saved state, estimate, human verification, and promise limits.

## 4. Draft a sponsorship proposal

Write a first sponsorship proposal and mark it **not sent**. The point is not to make yourself look bigger than you are. The point is to define what you can responsibly offer before anyone asks.

Include:

- who you are;
- who your current audience appears to be;
- where you publish;
- what topics you cover;
- what sponsors would fit;
- what you can offer now;
- what you cannot promise yet;
- what data you can share;
- what the sponsor must provide;
- what you will not promote.

Early terms you can offer might include one short video mention, one dedicated short video, one Xiaohongshu note, one WeChat group mention, a Douyin/Xiaohongshu bundle, a test collaboration, or a simple 7-day performance report.

Do not promise sales, follower growth, conversion, or platform reach. Do not let a sponsor control your voice or your family life.

Use this boundary:

> I can share honest content with my audience when the product or service fits my life and values. I do not guarantee sales, follower growth, or platform reach, and I do not promote products I would not want associated with my own family or public identity.

## 5. Consider a personal store on the video platforms

Also consider whether your video platforms should eventually have a small personal store attached to them. This could include Grace Mar products later, if the audience and product fit are real.

Do not open the store just because the platform makes it possible. First define what kind of product would honestly belong beside your content. A personal store should feel like a natural extension of your audience relationship, not a sudden sales shelf.

Possible early store directions:

- products you personally use and can explain honestly;
- small Grace Mar test products, once the business container is ready;
- Grace Gems items only if the product promise, fulfillment path, and review boundary are clear;
- digital or low-risk products that match your audience's questions;
- affiliate or sponsored products only when the relationship is disclosed and the fit is real.

Before opening anything, write a one-page store-readiness note:

- what would be sold;
- why it belongs with your account;
- who would buy it;
- what platform rules apply;
- who fulfills it;
- what can go wrong;
- what you must not promise;
- what evidence would make you wait.

This is not urgent. It is a CEO question to keep nearby: if your audience begins to trust you, what kind of product channel would deserve that trust?

## Keep the containers separate

Grace Gems is the product-review container.

Grace Mar Ops is the business-operations container.

The WeChat group is the audience-listening container.

The sponsorship proposal is the commercial-offer container.

The personal store is a possible owned-commerce container.

Do not collapse these into one vague project. The CEO work is to let each surface have a job.
