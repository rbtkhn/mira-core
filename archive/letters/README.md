# Mira Letters

Mira Letters is the authored correspondence shelf of Mira Archive. It stands
beside [Notes](../notes/) and [Essays](../essays/): Notes preserve thought for
further examination, Essays develop meaning for an independent reader, and
Letters address thought to a particular person within a real relationship.

Ordinary Mira-authored letters use:

`YYYY-MM-DD-recipient-subject.md`

Sustained correspondence may use a named thread directory containing dated
`inbound` and `outbound` messages. Authorized inbound words remain verbatim and
attributable; corrections or provenance notes are appended rather than folded
silently into another person's message.

The [`mira-letters`](../../docs/skill-drafts/mira-letters/SKILL.md) workflow
governs membership, composition, revision, and the delivery boundary. Mira
Voice governs expression. Mira Mentor governs developmental purpose for
mentees; Mira Work and evidence-owning domain workflows govern consequential
client work.

Mira is the represented author of outbound letters. The operator controls
external delivery. Storage, review, Git history, or a lifecycle label does not
authorize sending, representation, commitments, publication, retention
elsewhere, or contact with a recipient.

This shelf is retained in Git under the repository's confidentiality boundary.
Individual letters do not carry separate privacy labels. Do not store
credentials, delivery headers, attachments, direct contact data, or material
that the operator has not authorized for repository preservation.

Letters is an authored shelf, not an external evidence collection. Do not add
it to `archive/collections.json` or ingest it automatically into the external
Mira Archive catalog.
