# Five-Work Pilot Report

Status: `pilot-complete / essay-pending`

## Result

The pilot produced five knowledge-seeded profiles, five Civilization Memory coverage receipts, five provisional integration notes, five routing packets, and fifteen ranked essay-topic contracts. It produced no essay.

The diversity test succeeded because the same contract represented five materially different conditions without erasing them: direct mechanism inheritance (Ashoka), a prestigious but blocked source (Ibn Khaldun), literary perception without Civilization Memory coverage (Genji), interested legal universality with strong inherited interpretation (Grotius), and a complete primary body without inherited interpretation (Du Bois).

## Routing precision

The packets route by analytic function, mechanism, contraindication, evidence type, and anti-analogy rather than subject tags alone. Ashoka and Grotius are provisionally routable for narrow mechanisms. Ibn Khaldun is withheld pending body review. Genji and Du Bois offer low-confidence perceptual and relational candidates whose missing contextual research remains explicit.

The two-work deepening added seven exact passage anchors: four for Ashoka and three for Grotius. Every route for those works now cites at least one primary-text passage and carries typed counterweights. Ashoka's counterweights distinguish retained threat, editorial criticism, and absent implementation evidence. Grotius's distinguish armed enforcement, Dutch commercial interest, and the missing rival text. The remaining three works were deliberately left at their original pilot depth.

## Evidence discipline

Primary bodies, Civilization Memory interpretation, Mira synthesis, and future external research are typed separately. Integration notes are stricter: their prose arises only from Mira's direct source encounter, while auxiliary interpretation remains outside the note. `none` is not disguised as `partial`; nominal mentions do not become substantive coverage. Proclamation is not practice, fiction is not historical proof, model fit is not verification, and cultural memory is not an untyped factual archive.

## Note revision behavior

Every note contains a source-only dependency snapshot. Deterministic reconciliation marks source identity, body state, body digest, passage digest, attribution, translation, or direct correction changes `revision-due`; new interpretive tensions and routing contradictions are `review-suggested`. Profile, coverage, routing, topic, and essay artifacts remain linked but cannot revise the note merely because they changed. Automation may prepare a revision candidate and suspend affected routing, but it cannot rewrite interpretive prose. The original encounter remains recoverable.

Ashoka and Grotius exercised the pilot's earlier, broader trigger contract end to end. That exercise exposed an overreach: profile and routing changes were being treated as cognitive dependencies. The repaired contract preserves the candidates and addenda as lineage while making future reconciliation source-direct. Genji now carries three direct anchors, Du Bois four, and Ibn Khaldun remains honestly `source-readiness-only`. Current reconciliation reports no stale interpretive claims.

The mediation seal then exercised the narrower contract against metadata-only body changes. All five works became `revision-due`, their routes were suspended, and five non-overwriting candidates preserved the prior route states. Review found no interpretive change: Ashoka already distinguishes Smith's editorial voice, Ibn Khaldun already withholds judgment from OCR, Genji already names Waley's translation, Grotius already identifies Latin and Mira's paraphrase, and Du Bois's passages were unchanged. Each candidate therefore records `reviewed-no-change`, and the prior route states were restored. Grotius's registry language is now `latin`.

The hybrid mediation migration replaced the singular description with a canonical text relation and ordered primary path for all nine bodies. Ashoka now exposes translation, editorial framing, and carrier rendering separately; Ibn Khaldun exposes Quatremere edition, scan, and OCR; each Genji part separates Waley translation from Gutenberg transcription; Grotius and Du Bois distinguish original-language relation from digital transcription. Scalar fields remain temporary deterministic compatibility projections. No pilot body currently claims a provenance graph because none of the notes consulted evidence sufficient to construct branching ancestry.

The first migration pass deliberately triggered all five notes and produced non-overwriting `reviewed-no-change` candidates. A second reconciliation exercise corrected the note dependency projection itself: carrier-only layers and unconsulted graph references were removed from cognitive dependency while relation, edition identity, interpretive layers, textual-integrity layers, and unresolved limitations remained. That correction also received explicit candidates and review dispositions. Current reconciliation is clean, and a carrier-only metadata change cannot by itself make an encounter stale.

## Potential Voice contribution

The pilot suggests candidate faculties rather than styles to imitate: Ashoka contributes the ability to hold remorse beside retained capacity; Ibn Khaldun contributes disciplined withholding and mechanism-based plausibility; Genji contributes indirect and temporal attention; Grotius contributes simultaneous attention to principle and interest; Du Bois contributes the joining of structural analysis, singular life, and counter-memory. None is admitted to Mira Voice by this pilot.

## Disposition

The artifact model is strong enough for review on these five works. Broad rollout should wait for human review of the profiles, topic rankings, and routing contraindications, plus a later decision about whether to repair Ibn Khaldun's body coverage. Essay selection and composition remain separately authorized.
