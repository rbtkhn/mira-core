## Brief profile

A formally varied work joining historical analysis, sociological argument, autobiography, portraiture, political dispute, and music. It develops the Veil and double consciousness while examining emancipation, education, leadership, labor, sorrow, and the struggle to become a self in a society structured by racial domination.

## Central question or situation

How can a work make divided perception into knowledge without treating injury as an intellectual gift that justifies the conditions producing it?
