## Brief profile

A prolegomenon to universal history that asks how reports about society and rule can be tested against causal knowledge of social organization. It is associated with analyses of group solidarity, dynastic formation, urbanization, taxation, labor, education, and the recurrent corruption of historical transmission.

## Central question or situation

Can knowledge of how societies work discipline inherited reports without turning a powerful causal model into a universal cycle?
