## Scope

This batch evaluates the next ten selected Medieval authorities not already
covered by Edition Research Batches 01–02 or Portable Batch 03. It resolves
candidate edition routes only. It does not download files, mutate the registry,
or admit bodies.

## Recommended Portable Batch 05

The next download-and-inspection batch should prioritize:

1. Dante, Project Gutenberg 1000 and 8800.
2. Judah Halevi, Internet Archive `judahhalleviskit00judauoft`.
3. Gregory of Tours, Library of Congress / Gutenberg Brehaut 1916.
4. Maimonides, LOC Judeo-Arabic manifest plus Friedländer English.
5. al-Mas'udi, the nine-volume Arabic-French sequence as a ledgered stress
   case.
6. Thomas Aquinas, only if portable files and explicit reuse terms can be
   recovered before download.

Constantine VII, Psellos, the *Kebra Nagast*, and Domesday should remain
metadata-first until their stated rights or representation-layer gaps are
resolved.

## Evidence and Limits

- Twenty substantive sources were used, two per candidate.
- Repository rights statements remain source assertions, not independent legal
  adjudications.
- Web visibility is not portability.
- Historical translations and original-language editions are never treated as
  equivalent merely because both exist.
- Multipart works require explicit file and volume ledgers before completeness
  claims.

[`edition-research-batch-03.json`](edition-research-batch-03.json).

store, era indexes, staging area, commit history, remotes, Archive catalog, and
