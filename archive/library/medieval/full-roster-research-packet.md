## Evidence and Feasibility Rules

- Each candidate carries at least one institutional, scholarly, or catalogue identity/provenance route in the JSON manifest.
- A collection index or catalogue search establishes triage identity only; it does not prove file provenance, completeness, or reuse permission.
- `restricted`, `unknown`, and `not-located` routes remain metadata-only or body-research-incomplete.
- Aggregators may preserve discovery lineage but never become the upstream admission source.
- Composite, fragmentary, recension-bound, or derivative traditions retain those limits in their corpus boundaries and maturity ceilings.
- Scores guide selection but do not overrule a failed composition or authority-boundary gate.
