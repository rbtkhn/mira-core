## Decision Result

Batch 02 completes first-pass edition triage for the eight accepted pilot authorities not covered in Batch 01 and adds two Latin comparators, the Magna Carta tradition and Chaucer. It authorizes no registry mutation, download, private-body admission, completeness claim, or maturity advancement.

Three routes are strongest for a later file-inspection pass: Bede's 1907 Sellar translation, Chaucer's Skeat Middle English edition, and Kalhana's two-volume Stein edition. Magna Carta's 1215 National Archives surface is authoritative but cannot represent the later reissues. Procopius and al-Biruni have promising historical-edition routes requiring multi-volume reconciliation. Al-Tabari, Ibn Khaldun, Nizam al-Mulk, and Sima Guang remain restricted, incomplete, or non-portable.

## Authority Findings

### Procopius — *Wars; Buildings; Secret History*

The authority is admitted to research as three separate work bodies. Perseus confirms Dewing's bilingual 1914–1928 lineage and all eight books of *Wars*; this does not settle bulk reuse or cover the other two works. Preferred next action: inspect all five *Wars* volumes and locate separate *Buildings* and *Secret History* files. [Text surface](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A2008.01.0670) · [catalog](https://catalog.perseus.tufts.edu/catalog/urn:cts:greekLit:tlg4029.tlg001.opp-eng1)

### Bede — *Historia ecclesiastica gentis Anglorum*

Project Gutenberg 38326 is a strong English candidate: A. M. Sellar's 1907 complete five-book translation, based on Plummer's Latin and the Giles/Stevens translation lineage. A matching Latin body remains unresolved. Preferred next action: inspect the plain-text file and locate the exact Plummer Latin edition. [ebook record](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/38326) · [edition front matter](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/38326/38326-h/38326-h.html)

### Magna Carta textual tradition

The 1215 charter and its 1216, 1217, 1225, and 1297 reissues must remain separate. The National Archives supplies an authoritative 1215 translation and explicitly marks clauses retained or omitted in later issues; exact file-level reuse terms still require inspection. Preferred next action: build the reissue ledger before inspecting any body. [National Archives resource](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/education/resources/magna-carta/british-library-magna-carta-1215-runnymede/) · [translation PDF](https://cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk/documents/education/magna-carta/magna-carta-lesson3-source9.pdf)

### al-Tabari — *Tarikh al-rusul wa-l-muluk*

SUNY confirms a complete modern English series—thirty-nine translated volumes completed in 1999 plus a 2007 index—but it is restricted and not proposed for admission. A complete, lawful de Goeje-lineage Arabic set remains unresolved. Preferred next action: construct an Arabic volume ledger. [SUNY series](https://sunypress.edu/Books/S/Set-History-of-al-abari) · [BnF volume record](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb36183917z)

### Ibn Khaldun — *Muqaddimah*

The boundary is the introductory book of *Kitab al-Ibar*, not every later abridgment. Quatremere's 1858 Arabic edition is a historical candidate but its volumes and files have not been reconciled; Rosenthal's complete English remains restricted. Preferred next action: inspect the complete Quatremere set. [edition record](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1597257W/The_Muqaddimah_an_introduction_to_history?edition=key%3A%2Fbooks%2FOL3018348M) · [work record](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1597257W/The_Muqaddimah_an_introduction_to_history)

### Nizam al-Mulk — *Siyar al-muluk / Siyasatnama*

The received fifty-chapter text preserves a disputed thirty-nine-chapter core plus eleven additions. Iranica records both the weaknesses of Schefer's 1891–1897 edition and Darke's later manuscript-based revisions; the modern English is restricted. Preferred next action: inspect Schefer as a historical edition while encoding its non-equivalence to Darke. [work history](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/siar-al-moluk/) · [Darke bibliography](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/darke-hubert-seymour-garland-1/)

### al-Biruni — *Kitab al-Hind*

Sachau's two-volume 1910 *Alberuni's India* is a promising English route, but both volumes and their connection to Sachau's Arabic source edition require inspection. Preferred next action: reconcile both English volumes before making any complete-work claim. [book record](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL23270588M/Alberuni%27s_India.?show_page_status=1) · [work record](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1532648W/Alberuni%27s_India)

### Kalhana — *Rajatarangini*

The proposed boundary is Kalhana's eight books, excluding later continuations. Wikisource exposes Stein's 1900 two-volume edition and its textual apparatus, but the transcription is not proofread and must not silently replace the scans. Preferred next action: inspect both DjVu volumes and distinguish Sanskrit, translation, notes, and maps. [volume index](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Index:Kalhana%27s_Rajatarangini_Vol_1.djvu) · [contents](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Kalhana%27s_Rajatarangini_Vol_1.djvu/21)

### Sima Guang editorial tradition — *Zizhi Tongjian*

The principal chronicle is 294 juan; supplementary tables, critical analysis, later commentaries, and modern punctuation are separate layers. CText is useful for identity inspection but its terms do not authorize bulk republication, and no complete English translation has been located. Preferred next action: find a complete institutional scan with explicit reuse terms. [work record](https://ctext.org/datawiki.pl?if=en&remap=gb&res=176090) · [reuse limits](https://ctext.org/faq)

### Geoffrey Chaucer — *The Canterbury Tales*

Gutenberg 22120 is a strong Middle English candidate based on Skeat's edition. Because Chaucer left the work unfinished and manuscript orders vary, the body ceiling is edition-specific; the non-Chaucerian *Gamelyn* must remain excluded or explicitly disputed. Preferred next action: inspect the Gutenberg file and encode order and fragment boundaries. [Skeat ebook](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/22120) · [Lansdowne MS 851](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-002078368)
